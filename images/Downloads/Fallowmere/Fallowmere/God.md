

There are three gods in this plane. The first and eldest is the [[Maker]], absent God of Creation. The second is the [[Lord Reaper]], the God of Death and Justice. The third is the [[Trickster]], Goddess of Whimsy and Magic.

- **The Maker** has been absent since recorded history. Their touch is still felt in some places in the world, but they have returned to [[Revery]] and await humanity there.

- **The Trickster** and **The Lord Reaper** have been at feuding for almost a millennium, and are openly at war since the first [[Reaping Season]].

- [Clerics](Class) can source their power from **The Lord Reaper** or **The Maker**, although each deity has a different approach to endowing mortals with divine magic. The Maker's magic is unpredictable and reflexive. The Lord Reaper mediates his power through [aspects](Reaper%20Aspects.md).
