These are notable fairies encountered by players in [[Current day]].

- **Camulorix, Patron Fairy of the Gleam (deceased)**. Late Patron of the [[Rosewater|Witchwood]]. Camulorix reached power ahead of the disappearance of the [[Exiled Races]], following his victorious battle against one of their armies on the plain where the *Witchwood* now stands. Traces of this battle can be found in the forest still.
  Camulorix enjoyed friendly terms with the druid *Jenny*. His flower is chamomile.

- **Valeorix**, a lesser fairy of the Gleam. He dreams to become the new *Patron of the Gleam*, and is focused on obtained the [[Maker Blade]], which he believes will solidify his claim to the station. His flower is valerian. (Has become the New Patron of the Gleam)

- **Idunn, Patron Fairy of Youth and Summer**. She is a powerful fairy associated with the *Apples of Youth*, and open to pacts with mortals. She seeks to keep the [[Maker Blade]] out of her rivals' hands, and desires to destabilise the Fairy Domains of other Patrons. Her own *Fairy Domain* is the [[Hidden Orchard]].
