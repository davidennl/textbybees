#god 

**The Maker** is the absent [[God]] of Creation. They are the eldest of the gods, and reside in [[Revery]]. Being absent, there is no consensus on the nature, goals, or domains of this deity.

- [Maker clerics](Class) are persecuted by most civilised society, particularly regions where the [Justiciar Church](Church) is dominant, because they are considered to project meaning onto a god who wishes to have none. By worshipping **The Maker**, the Justiciars argue, the god is being forced back to the world.

- Some places, such as [Rosewater's hidden shrine](Rosewater), retain a touch of **The Maker**'s presence. They possess great power for transformation, and are feared by both [[Horrors]] and [[Fairies]] alike.
