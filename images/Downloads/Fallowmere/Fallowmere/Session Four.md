**Locations**
- *The Dead Stag*
Players find a dead stag on the forest path. It has been torn to shreds by ghouls. In the distance, the sound of many creatures can be heard. Your presence has been noted. (2 survival checks needed to outrun ghouls).

- *The Clearing*
A gorgeous little glade beside a stream, where the light always seems to fall slanted. Thousands of chamomile flowers bloom here, hosting minuscule fairies. The fairies can guide players to the marsh, but they will attempt to draw them into political machinations first. The death of a great fairy, after all, means a job vacancy has opened, and all the fairies are clamouring to become the new Patron Fairy of the Gleam. 

Delphine and Wickham are caught in a war of sprites, and they will attempt to coax the players into a promise of loyalty. Loyalty to one will mean slaying the other. The fairies have only 10HP and an armour class of 8, but killing a fairy will have consequences...

- *The Sunset Tree*
In the deep forest stands a huge oak tree. Its purple canopy is visible from a distance. If approached, players will note a flock of sprites playing on the tree’s bark. From the tree, the sky always looks to be at dawn or dusk.

There is also a green-hued metal hilt, hidden in the bushes (Perception DC 12). Players will assume it belongs to an underground goblin designer or maybe Empire craftsman, but the metal—a strange, rust-free alloy of Veridian—betrays a more foreign source (one of the Exiled races). A high history or arcana check (DC16) will allow players to discover Camulorix defeated an army here, founding his court on what was once a battlefield.

A pool of water flows at the roots of the tree. Drinking it allows a player to regain their spell slots, but they glow faint gold for the next 24 hours, acting as 'fairy fire'.

- *The Glittercourt*
Once a beautiful meadow where Camulorix held court, now a graveyard littered with dead sprites, shattered fairyglass furniture, and a single, splayed corpse. The corpse belongs to Camulorix himself. He lies before a crumbled throne, his frame shattered in many places.

His body has a shattered twilight chainmail, and a brittle crown of fairy-gold. There is a letter in Camulorix’s hand. It's a plea to Jenny to come to the forest: things have gone awry, and he suspects foul play. A Guardian has been infected, and there have been clashes with two undead.

Orry is, of course, Valeorix in disguise. The promise of vengeance he tries to get the players to accept ensures they will not only have to kill the undead horse, but also the wight rider Bertrade of Helmstadt herself, who has left the forest (act 2). His plan is simple: become the new Fairy lord of this realm. He originally wanted the Maker Blade as well, but will be satisfied with the players using it against the undead. He is not evil or good.

There are no hoof prints in the meadow: only a single footprint belonging to something undead but not ghoulish. A DC investigation 18 is required to determine this.

The players will, upon inspecting the area, be spoken to by a fairy with golden skin who sits by a tree, crying large, glossy tears and bleeding from a festering cut. Her name is Orry, a warmaiden of the Gleam, and she is mortally wounded. She will offer a boon to the party. In return for a promise of vengeance, the players will gain these boons permanently. The fairy can also, if persuaded, lift fairy curses.

- *The Marsh's edge*
The ground grows soft and slippery, puddles expanding to pools. Bran will see something here, in the distance. He will need to roll a perception check to determine who it is: the Horror he's been hunting.

The players will need to roll a stealth check (dc15) to pass through the marsh undetected. Should they fail, they will be harried by fairies and incur one level of exhaustion.

There will be quicksand next. Players will need to either all succeed on an athletics check (dc15), or risk drowning in quicksand. Once stuck, a DC15 strength check is required to escape. 3 fails = death.

The ground slopes upwards. Ahead are trees, gnarled and knotted. Pale Mist, a melancholy fairy, sits on a tree branch here. She will offer clues as to what happened here, and who the Horror is. Horse’s tracks will become visible, with boot prints beside them. A vision may be offered, for a price (Pale Mist wants something of value from each player. Something they hold terribly dear).

Arcana/investigation checks at 15DC are required to uncover details about Bertrade's final moments.
