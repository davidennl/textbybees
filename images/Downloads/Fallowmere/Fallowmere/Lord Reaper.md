#god

**The Lord Reaper** is the [[God]] of Death and Justice. He guards the gates to [[Revery]], judging all those who seek to enter. If rejected, mortal souls are cast back to the world, reincarnated until they live a just life.

- **The Lord Reaper** is mediated through divine Aspects. The three most influential Reaper Aspects are the [Judge](Reaper%20Aspects.md), the [Widow](Reaper%20Aspects.md), and the [Sentinel](Reaper%20Aspects.md).

- [The Reaping Seasons](Reaping%20Season.md) are The Lord Reaper's punishment to humanity for aligning with the [[Trickster]], with whom he feuds. [[Horrors]] are his agents in this war, although his divine Aspects, and followers, are conflicted on their allegiance to them.

- [Reaper clerics](Class)' abilities vary according to the Aspect to which they are dedicated.
