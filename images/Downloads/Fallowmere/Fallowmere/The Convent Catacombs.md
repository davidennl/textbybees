_A cramped network of burial passages beneath the Convent of the Pale Sisters_

## Overview

The catacombs consist of five interconnected passages radiating from a central chamber. The air smells of damp earth and something floral-sweet. There are places to hang torches on the wall. The ceiling is low (7 feet), and passages are narrow (10 feet wide). A PC can move from any passage to an adjacent one in a single turn.

**Coffin Teleport Mechanic:** When Davon drops to 35 HP or 18 HP, it uses Coffin Step as a reaction (doesn't cost its bonus action) and immediately hides inside an empty, closed coffin. Players must search coffins to find it. While hidden, Davon uses its turn to summon **Earthen Swarms** (see below). Players can search one coffin per action. Finding Davon allows attacks against it, but it remains in cover (+2 AC) until pulled out (Athletics DC 13) or the coffin is destroyed (AC 15, 18 HP).

---

## Central Chamber

A circular room with six alcoves, each containing a stone urn filled with ashes, one filled with mushrooms. Simple stone carvings of House emblems decorate the walls. Passages branch north, east, south, and west. East is gated shut by a locked door and a Widow's seal, but it's begun to fade.

**The Widow Seal:** A fifth passage leads down, but it's blocked by a heavy stone door. Carved into its surface is an old Widow seal—a stylized woman in funeral garb wearing a wedding veil, her hands folded in mourning. The door has no visible handle or keyhole. Whatever lies in the deeper levels of the catacombs remains sealed and inaccessible.

**Feature:** A knee-high hole in the eastern wall (behind an urn). Goblin tweens peek through and shout insults whenever PCs approach within 15 feet: _"Pooh, it stinks in here!" "Bet you can't even read!"_ The hole leads to goblin tunnels beyond the catacombs.

## North Passage: Ferdinand's Mausoleum

A 30-foot passage ending in a small stone building (15×15 feet) built _within_ the catacomb. The structure is covered in Rose DuLac reliefs—the roses are clearly aquatic, with stems disappearing into carved water.

**Mausoleum Interior:** A single marble sarcophagus on a raised dais. The lid shows Ferdinand as a handsome farmer holding wheat. Inscriptions cover all four walls:

> _"Here rests Yeoman Ferdinand, cursed in youth by the Lord of Briars for a debt unpaid. Thirteen years his harvests failed, until wisdom brought him to sacred waters. He lived four-score years more, loved by his children, blessed by his fields."

**Rose DuLac Details:** The carved roses have hollow centres inlaid with Fell oyster mother-of-pearl, giving them an eerie blue glow.

**Coffin:** Ferdinand's sarcophagus is sealed (Strength DC 15 to open). Inside: Ferdinand's well-preserved remains in farmer's clothes, a corroded sickle, and a small jade rose (worth 50 sp). **Davon can teleport here.**

### Additional Coffins (3)

- **Coffin 1 (closed):** Empty except for root tendrils. **Davon can teleport here.**
- **Coffin 2 (closed):** Contains a deceased priest's skeleton, rotted vestments, a copper holy symbol of the Judge.
- **Coffin 4 (closed):** Blacksmith's remains, a well-crafted iron hammer (still usable, 20 sp), leather apron. **Davon can teleport here.**

## East Passage: The Collapsed Way

A 40-foot passage hidden behind a locked door (DC10 lockpicking) that ends in a wall of collapsed stone and earth after 25 feet.

**Clue:** The collapse is relatively recent—fresh earth, broken masonry. A DC 12 Investigation check reveals the stones are carved with Maker iconography (sunburst symbols).

---

## East Passage: The Drowned Hall

A 35-foot passage with water damage—the floor is muddy and puddles collect in depressions. The walls weep moisture, and the air is cold and damp.

**Feature:** At the passage's end, a small natural spring bubbles up through cracks in the floor. The water is clear and cold. A DC 13 Nature check identifies it as connecting to an underground water table—not the river Dapple running through the village.

### Coffins (5)

- **Coffin 1 (closed):** Miller's skeleton, a leather purse with 8 silver pieces (water-damaged). **Davon can teleport here.**
- **Coffin 2 (closed):** Empty, but filled with water. **Davon cannot teleport here** (occupied by water).
- **Coffin 3 (open):** Young man's remains, a love letter to "Maribel" (mostly illegible).
- **Coffin 4 (closed):** Farmer's wife's remains, copper bracelet, dried flowers that crumble to dust. **Davon can teleport here.**
- **Coffin 5 (ajar):** Empty except for beetles and worms. **Davon can teleport here.**

---

## West Passage: The Goblin Border

A 30-foot passage that feels watched. Crude goblin symbols are scratched into some coffin lids—tween graffiti.

**Feature:** The knee-high hole mentioned earlier is clearly visible here in the south wall. If PCs block or investigate it, they hear scurrying and giggling on the other side. A Small creature could squeeze through (goblins use it to explore the catacombs).

### Coffins (4)
- **Coffin 1 (closed):** Village elder's remains, brass spectacles (bent), a journal listing harvest yields from 80 years ago. **Davon can teleport here.**
- **Coffin 2 (lid missing):** Empty. Recently ransacked by goblins—scattered bones everywhere.

## Earthen Swarms (While Davon Hides)

While Davon is hidden in a coffin, it uses its action each turn to summon insects and worms that harass the party.

**Earthen Swarm:** Each PC within 30 feet of Davon's hidden coffin must succeed on a DC 13 Dexterity saving throw or take 5 (2d4) piercing damage from biting insects and burrowing worms. Even on a success, the sensation is deeply unsettling.

**Finding Davon:** Once found and attacked, Davon emerges from the coffin on its next turn (unless pulled out earlier).

## Running the Teleport Mechanic

1. **Trigger:** Davon drops to 35 HP (first threshold) or 18 HP (second threshold)
2. **Reaction:** Davon immediately uses Coffin Step and hides (doesn't use its bonus action)
3. **Hidden Turn:** On Davon's turn while hidden, it summons Earthen Swarms (all PCs in range save or take 2d4 damage)
4. **Searching:** PCs can use their action to search one coffin. If they find Davon:
    - They can attack it immediately (Davon has +2 AC while in the coffin)
    - An ally can use an action to pull Davon out (Athletics DC 13)
    - They can attack the coffin itself (AC 15, 12 HP) to break it open
5. **Tension:** PCs must decide whether to search systematically or split up. The swarm damage incentivizes speed.
6. **Movement:** PCs can reach any adjacent passage in one turn (30 feet of movement), making the search dynamic.

**DM Tip:** Secretly roll to determine which coffin Davon hides in, or choose dramatically (Ferdinand's sarcophagus is thematically perfect for the second teleport).
