
**Reaping Seasons** are the [[Lord Reaper]]'s punishment for [[Magic]]. For about five-hundred years, every century the world enters a *fifth season* where the dead rise from their graves to complete *unfinished business*. 

- Reaping Seasons are responsible for the death of almost a million mortals, the political collapse of [[The Empire]], and the fragmentation of the Justiciar [[Church]]. [[Timeline|Six Reapings]] have taken place so far, and the Seventh is just beginning in [[Current day]].
