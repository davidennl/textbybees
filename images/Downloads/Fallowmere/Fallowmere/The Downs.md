The Downs stretch between the Witchwood and the Grim Mire. The Downs are flatland, with grazing animals and harsh winds from the Fallowmere keeping large plants from taking root. Grass stretches from horizon to horizon, gentle rolling hills and peat as far as the eye can see. It is home to sheep, red kites, and some wolves. Occasionally, slight valleys allow for forest to grow. The nobles or wealthy yeomen prefer to build their manors here.

The Imperial Road cuts right through the Downs on its way to Fenburgh. At the south-west, the Downs are largely uninhabited. To the west and north, they lead into the Grim Mire, growing boggy and mossy, purple with heath. To the north-east, however, the Downs are fertile and relatively dry, and farmhouses have been built to work the land. The farms are split between a handful of minor nobles, primarily the Vosvechts, the Vilani, and the Devreaux. A significant amount of the farmland is also owned by the [[Fenburgh]] Justiciar Church, and pay tithe to the Deaconess.

- The [[Roadside Horrors|Barrow Man]] wanders the Downs, burying its victims near the Grim Mire.
- There is no Fairy Domain on the Downs, but there are Windwalker Fae who harass travellers and steal one's voice.
- [[Gerrit's Farm]] is along the last stretch of the road to Fenburgh, and is owned by the Justiciar Church.
