
[[Fallowmere]]’s geology is dominated by the enormous lake which gave the kingdom its name. Once a sea inlet, the Fallowmere has been cut off from the ocean for several thousand years. The *Summer river* and the *Dapple* empty
into this lake, and over time its salinity has dissipated, giving rise to unique species.

- **The Fell Seal**
The only fresh-water seal species known to exist, the Fell Seal is a generally placid mammal that feeds on small fish and snoozes on island beaches. It has no natural predators, and is usually no threat to humans, but during mating season
they can be very aggressive. 
Some villages hunt the seals for their pelts, but the practice is contentious. Helmstadt forbids it after Abenay, Patron Fairy of the Fallowmere Lake, threatened a curse upon every mortal settlement to touch its waters.

- **Catarina’s Oyster**
Named after the Empress who first began extracting violet pearls from these unique fresh-water molluscs, the *Catarina Oyster* is vital to Fallowmere textile industry. Skilled tailors weave thousands of these miniscule pearls onto garments, as per the tradition of ‘river silk’ embroidery.

- **Sweet Herring**
A fresh-water species of the herring commonly found in the [[Map|Zephyr Sea]], this species of fish is also known as ‘Peasant’s Bread’. Easily caught and prosperous, herring is a key ingredient in most local dishes across Fallowmere. Many towns have their own fermenting recipes, and Fallowmere cuisine is infamous for its love of pickled fish.
