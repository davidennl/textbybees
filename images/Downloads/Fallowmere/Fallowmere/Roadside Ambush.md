## Willa - Desperate Refugee
_Medium humanoid (human), neutral_
**Armor Class** 10 (ragged clothes)  
**Hit Points** 4 (1d8)  
**Speed** 30 ft.

|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
|10 (+0)|10 (+0)|10 (+0)|10 (+0)|11 (+0)|12 (+1)|
**Skills** Persuasion +3  
**Senses** passive Perception 10  
**Languages** Common  
**Challenge** 0 (10 XP)

### Actions

**Improvised Club.** _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +2 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 2 (1d4) bludgeoning damage.
**Plead for Mercy.** Willa can use her action to beg for her life or explain her desperate situation. The party must succeed on a DC 10 Wisdom saving throw or have disadvantage on attack rolls against her until the start of her next turn.
### Tactics
Willa is malnourished and terrified. She'll threaten with her club but her hands shake. If injured at all, she drops her weapon and begs for mercy. She's more willing to talk than Pjotr.

## Pjotr - Desperate Refugee
_Medium humanoid (human), neutral_
**Armor Class** 11 (leather scraps)  
**Hit Points** 7 (1d8+3)  
**Speed** 30 ft.

|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
|12 (+1)|11 (+0)|12 (+1)|9 (-1)|10 (+0)|8 (-1)|

**Skills** Intimidation +1  
**Senses** passive Perception 10  
**Languages** Common  
**Challenge** 1/8 (25 XP)

### Actions
**Rusty Dagger.** _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +3 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 3 (1d4+1) piercing damage.
**Rock.** _Ranged Weapon Attack:_ +2 to hit, range 20/60 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 2 (1d4) bludgeoning damage.

### Tactics

Pjotr is more aggressive than Willa, driven by desperation to feed his family. He'll fight initially but surrenders if reduced to 3 HP or less, or if successfully intimidated (DC 10 Charisma check). If Willa is hurt, he immediately tries to protect her.

## Ghoul
_Medium undead, chaotic evil_
**Armor Class** 12  
**Hit Points** 22 (5d8)  
**Speed** 30 ft.

|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
|13 (+1)|15 (+2)|10 (+0)|7 (-2)|10 (+0)|6 (-2)|

**Damage Immunities** poison  
**Condition Immunities** charmed, exhaustion, poisoned  
**Senses** darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 10  
**Languages** Common  
**Challenge** 1 (200 XP)

### Actions

**Bite.** _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 9 (2d6+2) piercing damage.
**Claws.** _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 7 (2d4+2) slashing damage. If the target is a creature other than an elf or undead, it must succeed on a DC 10 Constitution saving throw or be **paralyzed** for 1 minute. The target can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success.

### Tactics
The ghoul is drawn by the scent of blood or the sounds of combat. It emerges from hiding (behind trees, from a ditch) and immediately attacks the nearest living creature. It focuses on isolated targets and uses its paralyzing claws strategically. The ghoul fights to the death.
