- **Rosewater Goblin Market:** 

These goblins ended up here after the Lange Meer Market dug a little too deep, accidentally opening one of the Deep Roads, and consequently obliterated by a Forgotten Beast. The Market barely survived, and the goblins at Rosewater are therefore very careful not to dig downward.

*Pebble the tradesgoblin* is a welcoming, slightly slimy tradesman, eager to make trades and unscrupulous about strange deals. He does not take gold or money, but instead insists on trades. He will demand an odd or magical item for any of his own, or ask the party to steal or retrieve an object in [[Rosewater]] he’s interested in (the key to the beekeeper’s shed, something from the Tower basement, one of Flandrina’s boots, the alehouse’s warding rune). 

He will also ask a favour: to take the goblin baby Beetle to a different Market. He will look like a human baby to everyone who hasn’t been in a Goblin Market before.
