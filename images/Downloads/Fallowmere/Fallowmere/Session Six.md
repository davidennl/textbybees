**Locations**
- _The Heart of the Marsh_  
    The climb ends abruptly. Before you lies a hollow at the crown of the hill, a drowned barrow where stagnant water has gathered into a black, motionless pool. The air here is a suffocating miasma—methane thick as oil, a stench of sulfur and decay that stings the eyes. The sky itself seems heavy, as though pressing down upon the earth.

No birds sing. The few trees that rise from the water are pale and skeletal, their bark sloughing away in strips like rotting skin. A hush holds the marsh, broken only by the gurgle of bubbles rising from the black water.

Then you see it. Standing waist-deep in the bog, still as a grave marker, is [[The Bog Horse]]. Gaunt, its flesh hangs like drenched cloth, a rotted saddlebag half sunk into the exposed spine. Around it three ghouls lurch clumsily, occasionally falling into the mud, their skin bruised and leatherlike.

---

Once the bog horse killed, any player with ghoul rot will be healed. Any active ghouls in the Witchwood or in Rosewater will perish. Investigation of one of the ghoul minions reveals an elegant knife with the inscription ‘Billiam’ on the hilt (The knife will sell for 1 gold, or nothing if returned to the Barleys).

A search of the horse's body reveals a rotted saddlebag whose contents have, thanks to the peat grave the creature soaked in for 6 centuries, held up surprisingly well.

There's a sealed letter, dated to Year 17 of the Third Era (92 before the Fallowmere rebellion, over 600 years ago). A DC12 History check is needed to determine this connection.

The letter seal is of the House of Morning, the Fallowmere royal line preceding the current one (Javilin). A particularly good perception check shows the seal icon—a stag walking over water—belongs to the royals when they were still the Governors of the Lake District pre-rebellion.

In the bog horse's side is an arrow with a barbed head. The style is unlike anything normally encountered, but a tiny hand is engraved in its side, and there is a touch of the Reaper's magic on it.

---

Once the party has finished inspecting the scene, a figure will appear from the mist. The fairy Orry, now without her wound. She will grow in size and frame, revealing herself to be none other than Valeorix, the _new_ Patron Fairy of the Gleam. He will raise his hands, and the Witchwood will cease being corrupted. On the spot where the bog horse died, valerian springs up. This is the new Gleam's official flower. Sprites rise from the soil, filling the air like fireflies.

Valeorix names the party Friends to the Gleam, meaning they can travel through the Witchwood unhampered. He will also extend their boons, provided they meet their daily activating condition and continue in pursuit of Bertrade.

The party can now return to Rosewater to collect their bounty.
