The Exiled Races revered virdium as fragments of the Maker's body. Through tremendous effort, they forged it into viridian—weapons and armor of unparalleled quality. The process required three stages: extreme heat generated by mass labor, compression through water pressure, and a final melting in which the metal could be worked. Light, flexible, devastating in combat, and infused with divine essence, viridian cut through both fae and undead with equal ease.

Five or six foundries existed across the continent, built beneath large bodies of water where pressure could be harnessed. Their locations were state secrets even during the height of the Empire. When they broke down, they were soon forgotten.

## Fallowmere

The kingdom takes its name from Lake Fallowmere. Scattered throughout are dozens of islands, most uninhabited. One of these was once called the Isle of Barend. It housed the underground Fell Foundry, now buried and forgotten. The island has since been renamed Queen's Isle, and cultists have made it their base of operations.

## Viridian
#playertools 

Jade-green metal from the Old Empire era. Exceptionally rare now—most examples are ancient relics or fragments. Known properties:

- Remarkably light and flexible
- Holds an edge better than any modern metal
- Unusually effective against both fae and undead
- The Old Empire built sentry towers from jade-green bricks that still stand across the continent, seemingly immune to erosion

How viridian was made has been lost to time. Scholars believe it involved some form of advanced metallurgy, but no foundries or production sites have ever been found.
