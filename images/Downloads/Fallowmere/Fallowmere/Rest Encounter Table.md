[[Rumour Table]]
##### Camp Encounters (d10)
_Roll once per long rest_

### 1. Distant Lights
The watch sees lights in the distance (1-2 miles away). Campfires? Will-o'-wisps? A village?

**If investigated (2 hour excursion):**
- Roll d6: 
1-2 = refugee camp (two refugees, Wilma and Durch)
3-4 = abandoned hamlet
5 = fairy lights (mischievous but harmless) 
6 = Horror lair: [[Travel encounter table|roll for information]].

**If ignored:**
- Lights vanish by morning
- No other effect

---
### 2. Fellow Travelers Request to Share Fire

1d4 travelers (refugees, merchants, or Sentinels) request to share camp for safety.

**They offer:** News, rumors (roll twice on Rumor Table), shared rations, companionship.

**Complication (d6):** 1-4: Genuine, no issues 5: One is a thief (DC 15 Perception to notice; steals 1d6 gold if undetected) 6: One is ill/injured, needs help

---
### 3. Night Sounds
The watch hears something in the darkness:

**Roll d6:**
1. Howling (wolves or worse)
2. Distant screaming (humanoid, then stops abruptly)
3. Heavy footsteps circling camp (never comes close)
4. Scraping/scratching sounds
5. Whispers in unknown language
6. Complete, unnatural silence

**Party must decide:**
- Investigate (risky, may find nothing or encounter danger)
- Fortify camp (advantage on Perception to detect approach)
- Wake everyone and move (lose some rest benefits)

**DM decides:** Real threat (30% chance of encounter) or just atmosphere/false alarm.

---
### 4. Sprite Visitors
Curious sprites approach camp, attracted by firelight or party member with fae connection.

**If entertained:** Share one rumour or warning about road ahead.
**If ignored:** Play harmless pranks (untie things, rearrange items, make phantom sounds).

---
### 5. Nightmare
One party member (random or watch) has vivid nightmare:

**Content relates to nearby Horror (roll d4):**
1. Ancient Exiles: Dreams of being hunted by tall, thin figures with too many teeth
2. Bat-creature: Dreams of flying/falling, something watching from above
3. Frozen Wight: Dreams of being frozen alive, unable to move or scream
4. Dread Rider: Dreams of being run down by wagon, unable to escape

**Wisdom save DC 12:**
- Success: Wake disturbed but functional
- Failure: Gain 1 level of exhaustion, disadvantage on first check after waking

**Purpose:** Foreshadows nearby Horror without direct encounter.

---
### 6. Weather Turns

Conditions worsen overnight:

**Roll d4:**
1. Heavy rain (disadvantage on Perception, everything wet)
2. Fog rolls in (visibility reduced, eerie atmosphere)
3. Wind picks up (fire difficult to maintain, cold)
4. Unseasonable cold (frost forms, unsettling)

**Effect:**
- May impact rest (DM discretion—Constitution saves to avoid exhaustion)
- Changes travel conditions next day
- Atmospheric tension

---
### 7. Scavenger Animals
1d4 wolves or 1d6 wild dogs approach camp, attracted by food smells.

**They're hungry and bold, but not mindlessly aggressive.**

**Options:**
- Intimidate them away (DC 12 Intimidation or Animal Handling)
- Fight (low-stakes combat)
- Feed them (costs rations, but they leave peacefully)

**If killed:** Each yields poor quality pelt (worth 5 silver in town).

---
### 8. Refugee Family Passes in Night
A small family (2 adults, 1-3 children) stumbles past camp, not stopping. Clearly exhausted.

**If party calls out:**
- They're terrified, nearly collapse with relief at seeing other people
- Fled their farm "tonight" after seeing something
- Can share one rumor or warning
- Desperately need rest but too afraid to stop

**Moral choice:**
- Offer to share camp (costs some rations, but humane)
- Let them move on (they may not survive)
- Escort them to safety (2-4 hour detour)

---
### 9. Sign of Horror Passage
The watch (or random party member waking for any reason) notices something wrong:

**Roll on Clue Table for random Horror—evidence that it passed within 100 yards of camp during the night.**

Party was lucky it didn't notice them, or it chose not to engage.

Creates tension: party _almost_ encountered something deadly.

---
### 10. Peaceful Night
No encounters. Party gets full rest.

**Atmosphere:** Stars bright, distant sprite lights pretty, sense of temporary safety.

**One party member has pleasant dream or moment of clarity (inspiration).**
