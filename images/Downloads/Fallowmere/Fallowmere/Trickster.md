#god

The youngest of three [gods](God), **The Trickster**'s domains are [[Magic]] and whimsy, and she is the only god who walks the world alongside mortals. Many stories exist about the Trickster's descent from Revery, and many more regale her many adventures since. She is an agent of chaos, oft disguised and impossible to pin down.

- The Trickster's children are [[Fairies]], although they do not obey or even feel great loyalty towards her. In fairness, neither does she.

- The Trickster resides at an unknown location. Some speculate she lives across the [Zephyr Sea](Map), in the land of the [[Sea People]]. Others imagine she has a pocket realm, or that she retires to [[Revery]] in between her travels.

- The Trickster has been at war with her brother, the [[Lord Reaper]] for almost seven hundred years. While she does not command any specific armies or organisations against the Reaper's many agents, she is said to pull strings indirectly, bending the conflict to her own unknowable purposes.
