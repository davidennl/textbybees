
Usually, the reference to 'Church' points to the [[Reaper Aspects|Justiciar Church]], which follows the teachings of the **Judge**, an aspect of the [[Lord Reaper]] focused on justice, punishment, and law. *The Justiciar Church* has existed as long as recorded history, having had its original seat of power in [[The Empire]]'s first and greatest capital, High Valdor.

Following the [[Fallowmere Rebellion]], the Church's authority split between Archbishops, each representing a politically independent state.

- While much of the Justiciar Church's power has been lost to political fragmentation in the past five hundred years, it remains the dominant [[Lord Reaper]] sect. It is closely tied to secular authorities, and strives to support their consolidation of power.

- *Justiciars* have been known to operate in the shadows. In [[Fallowmere]], a Justiciar cell called [[The Blind Hand]] operates in secret on the Queen's Isle.
