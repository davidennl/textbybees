##### Travel Encounters (d12)
_Roll once per day of travel_

[[Rumour Table]]

### 1. Refugee Family

A family of 4-6 farmers with a handcart piled with belongings trudges along the road. They're exhausted, hungry, and jumpy.

**They fled from:** *Roll d6: 1-2: nearby farmstead, 3-4: village closer to Witchwood, 5-6: hamlet near Grim Mire*.

**They can share:**
- One rumour (1d4. 1 - Frozen Corpse, 2 - The Dread Carriage, 3 - the long skeletons, 4 - The Bat Creature)
- Desperate plea for food or medicine (feverish child)

**Complication (d6):** 
- 1-4: No complication, just desperate people. 
- 5: They're being followed by something (they don't realize). Party spots it with DC14 Perception.
- 6: They carry infection/sickness. Will spread to party without precautions.

---

### 2. Abandoned Farmstead

A small farm sits empty beside the road. Door hangs open, chicken coop is broken, crops trampled or withered.
**Investigation DC 12:** Signs of hasty departure 2-3 days ago. No blood or combat.

**Loot:** 1d4 days of rations (if party searches thoroughly), farming tools, maybe a hidden stash (DC 15: 2d10 silver).

**Complication (d6):** 
- 1-3: Truly abandoned, safe to rest/resupply 
- 4: 1d4 wild animals (wolves/dogs) have moved in 
- 5: Squatters (1d6 desperate refugees) who may be hostile if surprised 
- 6: One of the Horrors passed through recently (Clue 1d4: 1 - Frozen Corpse, 2 - The Dread Carriage, 3 - the long skeletons, 4 - The Bat Creature)

---
### 3. The Merchant's Warning

A lone merchant on horseback gallops past, barely slowing. He shouts a warning as he passes:

**Roll d4** (1d4. 1 - Frozen Corpse, 2 - The Dread Carriage, 3 - the long skeletons, 4 - The Bat Creature)

**If party tries to stop him:** DC 15 Persuasion. He's terrified and won't elaborate much, but confirms: he's racing to Rosewater, saw _something_ on the road, and isn't waiting to see it again.

---

### 4. Roadside Shrine (Safe)

A small shrine to the Lord Reaper sits at a crossroads. Fresh offerings suggest someone maintains it.

**Offerings:** Wildflowers, a few copper coins, a carved wooden figure.

**Effect if party leaves offering or prays:**
- Next travel encounter rolled is #12 (Safe Passage)
- OR one party member gains inspiration

**Hidden:** DC 14 Investigation finds a hollow in the shrine base containing a traveler's cache: dried herbs (healing poultices worth 15 silver), a small flask of spirits, and a note: _"For those who need it. —Sister Mercy"_

---

### 5. Sprite Mischief

A cluster of sprites (8-12) swarms the party, pulling at clothes, stealing small objects, making irritating sounds.

**They want:** Something shiny, a story, a song, or for someone to play with them.

**If party indulges them (5-10 minutes):**
- Sprites share one useful piece of information (roll on Rumor Table)
- Point out hidden danger ahead.

**If party is hostile:**
- Sprites create minor inconveniences for rest of day (untied boots, hidden items, phantom sounds)
- Disadvantage on next Stealth check.

**If party ignores them:**
- Sprites grow bored and leave, but steal one small non-valuable item

---
### 6. Fellow Travelers - Pilgrims

A group of 3-5 Justiciars in travel robes walks the road, heading from Fenburgh.

**Social encounter—they can share:**
- News from Rosewater or other villages
- Offer of healing (one _cure wounds_ among the group)
- Two rumours (roll on Rumor Table)

**One of them:** Is a former resident of the Downs. Can share specific information about *The Long Night* inn, or describe the Vilani manor.

---
### 7. Cold Winds
As evening approaches, the empty farmland feels increasingly hostile. Wind howls across open fields. No lights anywhere.

**All party members make DC 12 Wisdom save:**
- **Failure:** Feel profound unease. Disadvantage on Perception checks until they make camp or find shelter
- **Success:** Notice specific unsettling details (roll on Clue Table for random Horror—they sense its presence in the region)

**During night travel/camp in the Downs:**
- Sounds carry for miles (howls, creaks, distant screams—real or imagined?)
- Advantage for anything trying to spot the party
- No natural cover

**Lesson:** The Downs are _exposed_. Travelers should reach The Long Night or find shelter.

---

### 8. Corpse on the Road

A body lies in the road ahead. Fresh (1-2 days old) or old (weeks)?

**Investigation DC 12 reveals:**
- Fresh: Killed by (roll d4: 1-violence/weapons, 2-animal attack, 3-exposure/starvation, 4-specific Horror)
- Old: Been dead for weeks, surprisingly hasn't risen (yet)

**On person:** A few personal effects. Letter to a loved one. 2d6 copper.

**Moral choice:**
- Bury properly (takes 30 minutes, grants inspiration to character who digs)
- Leave it (risks it rising later, weighs on conscience)
- Loot and move on (efficient but callous)

**Danger:** If party spends more than 10 minutes here and rolls 1-2 on d6, something has noticed them (wandering undead or predator).

### 9. Horror Encounter - Roll d6

**1-2: The Ancient Exiles (Skeletal Trio)** Three skeletal figures with elongated frames and pointed teeth emerge from the trees. Two cast spells.
_Encounter begins at medium range. Spellcasters try to disable party while warrior engages._
**Stats:** Use your prepared statblocks

**3-4: Bat Creature** 
Travelling through wooded area (near Witchwood edge or approaching the County of Vilan):

Screeching from above. Party members must make DC 14 Perception to spot her before she attacks.

_She swoops down, attacks, and retreats to canopy. Hit-and-run tactics._

**Stats:** Use your prepared statblock

_Slow but relentless. Area around it is frigid._

**Stats:** Use your prepared statblock

---
**6: Dread Rider's Wagon** The sound of creaking wheels. A skeletal horse pulls an ornate wagon. Inside sits a noble skeleton in fine clothes.

_Wagon attempts to run party down, then noble skeleton emerges for combat._

**Stats:** Use your prepared statblock

### 10. Ominous Clues
Party doesn't encounter the Horror directly, but finds evidence of its presence:

 (1d4. 1 - Frozen Corpse, 2 - The Dread Carriage, 3 - the long skeletons, 4 - The Bat Creature)

---
### 11-12. Safe Passage

The road is clear and travel is uneventful.

**Party may:**
- Travel at increased speed (save 1-2 hours)
- Have peaceful time for RP, planning, or character moments
- Regain 1d4 HP from peaceful travel

**Optional:** Distant sprite lights in woods, beautiful sunset, or other peaceful imagery. Brief respite from Reaping dread.
