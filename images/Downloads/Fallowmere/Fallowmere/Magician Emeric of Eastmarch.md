
One of the first and most influential magicians, Emeric of Eastmarch formalised much of what modern wizardry is built on. He devised sprite-flasks as the means to use [[Magic]] in a replicable, controllable way.

- He was burnt at the stake in [[The Empire|High Valdor]] following the publication of the *Eastmarch Tome of Magicians*, in the [[Timeline|year 430 before the Reaping]].
