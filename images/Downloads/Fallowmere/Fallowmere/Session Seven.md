**Hooks**
- *Continue in Rosewater*
- The Goblins still want someone to take their baby to the Fenburgh goblin market.
- The nuns are worried about something coming up from the catacombs.
- The Rosewater forge is cold and unused, and yearns for a flame.
- Gerrit is having trouble with his sheep

- *Head to Fenburgh*
	Flandrina is determined to join the party to [[Fenburgh]], where she can board a ferry to Redstone Keep or Helmstadt. Sigrid will also be amenable to joining (ideally one or the other).

	It will be a five day journey to Fenburgh by carriage, three if one takes the shortcut through **the Witchwood**. 
	- Night 0: Random Rest Roll
	- Night 1: The party reaches the Imperial Roadside inn [[The Long Night]]. 
	- Night 2: Random rest roll at the edge of **the Downs**
	- Night 3: The party rests at Vilani Manor, triggering the [[Vilani Vampyr]] arc.
	- Night 4: Random rest roll at the edge of the [[Grim Mire]].
