
The Lord Reaper, embodying one of mortal men's greatest fears, has inspired many mediating aspects. Three of the most influential are:

- *The Judge*: A middle-aged man wearing a blindfold and simple robes, this aspect mediates justice, punishment, and law. His [[Church|Justiciar Church]] is strictly hierarchical and closely tied to secular powers. 

- *The Widow*: A young woman in a funerary veil, this aspect intercedes in matters of community, restorative justice, and loss. Her convents, such as the [[Rosewater|Rosewater Convent]], gather knowledge and serve their communities in practical ways.

- *The Sentinel*: An old woman in black plate, wielding an ebony spear and kite shield. This is the aspect of honour, just war, and righteous fury. Her followers congregate in keeps such as [[Map|Fort Solitude]], and test themselves against [[Horrors]].
