#playertools 

A general note on magic: in Fallowmere, magic is less something you do and more something done *to* you. [Magicians, sorcerers and witches](Class) exist, certainly, but theirs is far from a precise or replicable art. This is for two reasons. The first, magic is a relatively recent addition to the world. It has existed in its current form for less than [a thousand years](Timeline). Secondly, magic is the domain of the [[Trickster]], and she is not prone to reliability. 

Any spell requires The Trickster's participation, although not necessarily directly. You can convince a [fairy](Fairies) to channel it for you. Greater fairies can fuel advanced magics, while for “regular” spells, sprites suffice.

Using magic against fairies is risky. As a reaction, a fairy being targeted by a levelled spell that succeeds to hit can force the caster to roll for *Whimsy (D20)*. Failing the Fairy's spellcasting save causes the spell in question to be affected.  The result depends on the fairy:

-	The spell reflects back upon the caster
-	The spell is an illusion
-	The spell splits between the target and a random creature
-	The spell affects everyone
-	The spell turns into a 3rd level fireball, centred on the caster
-	The caster’s gravity is inverted for 1d4 turns.
-	The caster can only breathe underwater for 1d4 turns
-	The caster loses all spell slots of that level
-	The caster sinks into any surface not made of stone for 1d4 turns.
