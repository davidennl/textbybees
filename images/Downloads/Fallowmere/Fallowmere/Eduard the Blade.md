#npc [[Vilani Vampyr]]

Medium humanoid (human), lawful good. A weathered Blade who assists in compiling the Blade Order's Bestiary. He carries a worn journal and a bandolier of healing draughts. He has played (and lost) many card games to Mae during Blade gatherings.

**Armor Class** 16 (chain mail)  
**Hit Points** 26 (3d10 + 6)  
**Speed** 30 ft.

| **STR** | **DEX** | **CON** | **INT** | **WIS** | **CHA** |
| ------- | ------- | ------- | ------- | ------- | ------- |
| 16 (+3) | 12 (+1) | 14 (+2) | 10 (+0) | 13 (+1) | 8 (–1)  |

**Saving Throws** Str +5, Con +4  
**Skills** Athletics +5, Medicine +3, Survival +3  
**Senses** passive Perception 11  
**Languages** Common

### Abilities
**Second Wind (1/Rest).** As a bonus action, Bernard regains 1d10 + 3 hit points.

**Combat Potions (3/Day).** As a bonus action, Bernard can toss a healing potion to an ally within 20 feet. The ally can drink it immediately as a reaction, regaining 1d4 + 2 hit points.

### Actions
**Longsword.** Melee Weapon Attack: +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 7 (1d8 + 3) slashing damage.
