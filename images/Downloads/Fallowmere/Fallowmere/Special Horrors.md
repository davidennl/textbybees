These are [[Horrors]] with exceptional powers. The source of this enhanced strength is unknown, but it appears related to their *unfinished business*. Some Horrors of this calibre openly further the [[Lord Reaper]]'s goals, but others have their own, personal missions. Some of the Horrors encountered by players in [[Current day]] are:

- **Old Mabel**. Raised after a disrespectful burial rite by the mortician [[Quentin]], Old Mabel has been travelling through [[Fallowmere]] with uncertain goals. On the road to Fenburgh she meets [[Bertrade the Wight]], who tells her about the [[Deep Roads]]. Knowing her *unfinished business* lies somewhere in these Roads, Old Mabel joins her new mistress on her quest to avenge her death.
  
- **The Bog Horse**. Bertrade's horse. Raised after [[Bertrade the Wight]] abandoned it on her quest, the Bog Horse roams the marshes north of the Witchwood. It is the source of [[Ghoul Rot]], which has been infecting [[Rosewater]] and its citizens.

- **Bertrade the Wight**. Six centuries before [[Current day]], there was a plot to request Vesper's assistance in a [[Fallowmere Rebellion]] against the Empire, and a letter was drafted. But its bearer, a scout called Bertrade of Helmstadt, was intercepted. She and her horse were drowned in the [[Fairy Domains|Witchwood]], and her pursuers—acolytes of [[The Blind Hand]], a Lord Reaper cult that, believing the Rebellion to destroy the [[Church]] and its centralized authority, sought to prevent it by any means necessary.
  
  She rose from the pool in the Witchwood as an exceptionally powerful Horror. The injustice of her murder, brought about by the [[Lord Reaper]]'s own followers, demands retribution. Her horse was lame, one of its legs shattered. While she left on foot to pursue the descendants of those who wronged her, the undead horse remained, killing fairies with its mere undead presence, feeding on local sheep, unbearably and incomprehensibly lonely and grief-struck. It came upon a boy trying to become a Blade and bit him, causing the Berl to turn into an undead thrall. When One-Eye Frederick and his team were ambushed, they were likewise infected.
