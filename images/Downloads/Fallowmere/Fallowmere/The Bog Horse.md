Large undead, chaotic evil. Bertrade's dead horse haunts the shallow grave where their bodies lay for six centuries. Ghouls linger nearby, staggering lifelessly. 
**Armor Class** 13 (rotting hide)  
**Hit Points** 85 (10d10 + 30)  
**Speed** 40 ft., swim 20 ft.

| **STR** | **DEX** | **CON** | **INT** | **WIS** | **CHA** |
| ------- | ------- | ------- | ------- | ------- | ------- |
| 18 (+4) | 12 (+1) | 16 (+3) | 5 (–3)  | 12 (+1) | 10 (+0) |

### Actions
**Multiattack.** The Horror makes two attacks: one Hoof and one Bite. 

**Hoof.** Melee Weapon Attack: +6 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 13 (2d8 + 4) bludgeoning damage. 
**Infectious Bite.** Melee Weapon Attack: +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 11 (2d6 + 4) piercing damage. On a hit, the target must succeed on a DC 13 Constitution saving throw or contract Ghoul Rot (see below). 

	Ghoul Rot. Each turn on the afflicted creature's turn, they must succeed on a DC 15 Wisdom saving throw. On a failure, they are overcome with ravenous hunger and cannot move or attack until the start of their next turn. On a natural 1, they attack the nearest allied creature in a frenzy. If a creature is infected twice, they cannot regain hit points by any means.

### Legendary Actions
The Bog Horror can take 1 legendary action, only at the end of another creature's turn. 
**Move.** The Horror moves up to half its speed. · 
**Hoof.** The Horror makes one Hoof attack.

### Lair Actions
On initiative count 20 (losing initiative ties), the Bog Horror can take one lair action (it can use each lair action only once per combat): 
**Reaper's Touch.** All magic falters. Until the next round, creatures within 60 feet cannot cast spells or benefit from magical healing.
**Undying Bond.** The Horror restores 20 hit points to one of its ghoul minions. If the ghoul is at 0 hit points, it is resurrected with 20 hit points instead.

# Mud Ghoul (x2)
Medium undead, chaotic evil **Armor Class** 12  
**Hit Points** 22 (5d8)  
**Speed** 30 ft.

| **STR** | **DEX** | **CON** | **INT** | **WIS** | **CHA** |
| ------- | ------- | ------- | ------- | ------- | ------- |
| 14 (+2) | 12 (+1) | 10 (+0) | 7 (–2)  | 10 (+0) | 6 (–2)  |
### Actions
**Claw.** Melee Weapon Attack: +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 7 (2d4 + 2) slashing damage. If the target is a creature, it must succeed on a DC 13 Constitution saving throw or contract Ghoul Rot.

### Death Burst
When the ghoul dies, it explodes in a spray of acidic mire. Each creature within 5 feet must succeed on a DC 13 Dexterity saving throw, taking 7 (2d6) acid damage on a failed save, or half as much on a success.
