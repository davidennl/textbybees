#playertools	

Warriors, barbarians and rogues inhabit the world of Fallowmere just as they do any other, but some vocations are uniquely modulated by the nature of [[Magic]] in this land.


- **Magician/Witch (Wizard)**
Everyone knows magic requires [[Fairies]], but few know which type of fairy, caught at which time of day, is required for a specific spell. You, understanding the importance of such things, have studied sprites and their properties. Provided you have a long rest to prepare, you can cast spells by releasing sprites you've trapped in vials the night prior. Using sprites for magician's magic does not kill them, but neither are they very amused by your manipulation of their free will.


-	**Sorcerer/Sorceress (Warlock)**
Everyone says never to make a deal with a [Fairy patron](Special%20Fairies.md). It's dangerous, they say. Fairies will trick you, they say. Your soul will be bound to Oblivion by a furious Lord Reaper, they say. You knew better. What's a little deal made between friends? You've signed away your soul to a Fairy. In exchange, you can tap into its magical power. Small print? Never heard of it!


-	**Fairborn (Sorcerer)**
A term for anyone whose essence was altered by the [[Fairies]] in infancy. Some are begotten by fairy lovers; others are changelings raised among humans. Fairborn vary in the type of magic they can cast, the fairies with which they haveallegiance, as well as the extent to which their appearance is non-human, although it is usually subtle. Their mixed heritage is what allows them to persuade sprites to assist them with casting spells.


-	**Cleric**
You've dedicated yourself to a god, from whom you source a Divine power. Several gods are willing to accept clerics.

*Justiciar Cleric*: The most common cleric found across Fallowmere, these clerics are dedicated to the [[Lord Reaper]]'s [Judge](Reaper%20Aspects.md) aspect. *The Judge*, represented as a man of middle age, wearing simple robes and a blindfold, is dedicated to justice, law, rulership, and punishment. [The Justiciar Church](Church) is a centralized institution spanning most of the continent. Each province/kingdom has their own Archbishop, and several influential Deacons. 
Most people acknowledge the Judge's power, no matter their opinion on his ethos. He simply is. Justiciar clerics act as community judgers, jailors and executioners. They can also be found in administrative positions, tax collection and other government functions. 

Common cleric domains are grave, order, death, trickery, and knowledge. 
Justiciar clerics are the most ambivalent about Horrors, seeing them as a terrible but righteous punishment for humanity's wicked ways.

*Widow Cleric* The second largest religious order, they pledge themselves to [the Widow](Reaper%20Aspects.md). She's an aspect of the [[Lord Reaper]] represented as a maiden in a funerary veil. Hers are the domains of family, peace, community and mercy. Her faithful congregate in convents and monasteries across the continent, and act largely independently of one another, led by a convent Mother or monastery Father. Most Widow clerics are trained in basic medicine, the production of food, and perform acts of care in their community, ranging from mortician to brewer. Like Justiciars, they compile their knowledge in libraries, making them valuable enclaves of literacy. 

Common Widow cleric domains are peace, nature, life, and twilight. 
Most Widow clerics abhor [[Horrors]]. They seek non-violent ways of helping them pass on.

*Sentinel Cleric*: The Sentinel Order was founded by Emperor Ioric IV shortly after the third Reaping, when it became evident [Reaping Seasons](Reaping%20Season.md) would not be ceasing any time soon, and the [Justiciar Church](Church) proved unable to adequately respond to [[Horrors|Horror]] attacks. [The Sentinel](Reaper%20Aspects.md) is an aspect of the [[Lord Reaper]] represented as an old woman in full black plate. Her domain is the battlefield, and hers are the rules of war, honour, military prowess and righteous anger. She holds an ebony lance in one hand, a kite shield in the other. Her followers travel freely, seeking out injustice and righting it. The Order trains its members in fortified keeps, with each keep wary of its peers and secretive about its techniques. Sentinels have hidden safehouses across most of the continent. They can be found employed by rulers to enforce law, combat monsters and horrors, and retrieve assets.

Common Sentinel cleric domains are War, Forge, Order.
Sentinel clerics see Horrors as challenges sent by the Reaper. Overcoming these challenges will improve humanity, as well as the cleric themselves.

*Maker Cleric*: A rare sight in Fallowmere, although more common in the coastal regions of the Empire and the fertile plains of Oswick, Maker clerics vary in their beliefs and practices. All they have in common is their dedication to the [[Maker]], the first of the gods, who awaits mankind in [[Revery]]. **The Maker** is mysterious, absent and ephemeral. As such, Maker clerics are concerned with creation itself, the inherent beauty of the natural world, and the return to animality. 

Common domains are nature, twilight, tempest, light and arcana.
Maker clerics have no unified opinion on [[Horrors]]. They range from rejection on the basis of undead not being part of original creation, to worship.


-	**Bard**
Bards have gained unique access to magic through a boon from a [Patron fairy](Special%20Fairies.md). Usually, bards are created when a greater fairy blesses a lost child or traveller instead of cursing them. Like magicians, bards use sprites for their spells, although they coax sprites with song or music, rather than trapping them. A bard will always be accompanied by a few curious sprites, making them versatile spellcasters even in areas without them. Bards tend to avoid rural areas, as their affinity to fae is frowned upon, but some [[Songstress Guilds]], formed in cities where bards naturally congregate, send out members to collect new songs and stories.


-	**Druid/Ranger**
Druids draw on sprites for their magic but convince them to aid not on an individual basis, but with the assurance that the druid/ranger's magic will serve to protect [[Fairy Domains]]. For instance, instead of the Patron Fairy of Spring, their power would come from the inherent sanctity of that greater fairy's domain. Druids often serve as the protectors of fairy realms, guarding its borders from woodcutters or herbalists. As such, they are rarely encountered in the civilised world, preferring to inhabit the wild places where fairies dwell.


-	**Blades and Paladins (Paladin)**
The first Blades were those created by a [mysterious fairy Patron](Special%20Fairies.md) in the [year 191](Timeline) to help humanity survive the Reapings, but the principle of their creation—an oath spoken before a sprite, who binds it magically—holds true for other Paladin oaths as well. 

Paladins—except for [[Blades]]—are not beholden to the [[Lord Reaper]] or the [[Trickster]], but to a personal truth so profound it binds the very fabric of reality. 

Paladin magic uses fairies to be cast, but it is unique in that it gains their collaboration not through persuasion or allegiance, but by strength of conviction. Sprite oaths are binding in ways few understand, and breaking them can cause madness, curse, or even unmaking.
