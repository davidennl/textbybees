#playertools 

**Session 1 – Arriving in Rosewater**
The characters met at the crossroad, joined Emeric on the journey to [[Rosewater]]. On the way they fought a group of ghouls, and then they entered the village. They booked rooms at the merchant’s stop, where they found a Blade, Sigrid, dying of an infected wound. They went to the alehouse to investigate what was happening in Rosewater, discovering that its direly affected by the [[Horrors]]. On the way back, Caset drunkenly asked the mayor about the [[Viridian]] tower, and was invited to come and discuss the bounty the next morning.

**Session 2 – The Goblin Market and Convent**
The party awoke to find Emeric gone, and Sigrid rapidly deteriorating. With a combination of fairy and Widow [[Magic]], they managed to slow the ghoulification process. They spoke with Marion and then headed to the edge of the Witchwood to investigate. Both a spell to lure out fairies and a scouting venture by Fox failed to reveal any information. The party headed towards the convent, passing by the Rosewater cemetery on their way. There they encountered the secret [[Goblin Market]]. Bran bought a pair of shoes of protection there, but they refused any other items (or a goblin baby). At the convent, the party spoke with Mother Endurance, receiving from her a bottle of sunshine against the ghouls. They also spoke with Sister Scrupulous, who requested they go visit the druid Jenny for chamomile. The party returned to the edge of the Wood, entering Jenny's shack. There they found a journal, in Druid Glyphs, which told of a mysterious woman seen by Jenny in the marsh. Then, after being bitten by something in the Wood, Jenny locked herself in her basement to prevent herself from hurting anyone. The party realize a knocking is coming from the shack's basement door.

**Session 3 – The Maker's Sword**
The party confronted Jenny the ghoul in her basement. After killing her, they located Jenny's invitations from the Fairy Camulorix, a plea from the Fairy Valeorix to retrieve a magic sword from beneath the tower, and many spoiled herbs. Bran took a sample of the chamomile hanging from the ceiling, which had turned to black goo. Mae retrieved a magical pendant gifted to Jenny by [[Special Fairies|Camulorix, Patron Fairy of the Gleam]]. A map revealed a hidden entrance to the basement of the green tower in Rosewater. The party returned to the convent to bring word of Jenny's death, earning them potions from Sister Scrupulous. They discovered Lord Camulorix is the Patron fairy of the Gleam, and the lord of the Witchwood. Then the party returned to Rosewater to discuss the Horror bounty with the town mayor, Albert. They also checked in briefly to make sure Sigrid hadn't turned into a ghoul yet. She was still alive. Albert allowed them to inspect the tower, and Mae used Jenny's pendant to unlock the hidden mechanism in the floor. In the basement was a secret [[Maker]] Shrine and many scrolls. Caset, who'd been promised the secret to forging Viridian was hidden away here, realized to his horror that all the scrolls had decayed, and that he'd been tricked. He retrieved the [[Maker Blade]] from the shrine, and the party decided it was time to enter the Witchwood.
Upon Entrance, three party members fell to a fairy curse. Bran got 10, Mae got 6, and Fenni got 9.  

**Session 4 - The Glittercourt**
The party ventured into the Witchwood, narrowly avoiding a pack of ghouls thanks to Fenni’s guidance. In a chamomile clearing, they encountered the fairies Lord Wickham and Lady Delphine. Bran pledged his blade to Wickham but refused to strike Delphine, breaking his word—Wickham vanished in fury.

At the Glittercourt, they found devastation: shattered fairy glass, Lord Camulorix dead upon his throne, and signs of undead but not ghouls. A wounded fairy paladin, Orry, revealed the Court had been ambushed by two undead now gone from the forest, while a third lingered in the marsh. She charged the party with vengeance and blessed them with boons. After this, they left for the marsh. On the way, they took a short rest by the side of the forest path, and each had a waking dream. After awaking with gasps, the party walked on, coming upon a massive magical tree, perhaps the heart of the late Camulorix's power. As they prepared to rest, Mae found an ancient green-silver sword hilt on the ground. Digging deeper, they found more arrowheads and shards of that same metal. They realised then that the Witchwood stands over what was once an enormous battlefield.

**Session Five - Meeting Lily**
The party comes upon a clearing, meeting there again the fairy Lord Wickham, who had ensnared another unfortunate soul, the performer Lily Thorne, into dancing for him. After they shooed Wickham, Lily explained she'd taken a shortcut to Fenburgh through the Witchwood, only to be terribly lost. They were then ambushed by ghouls. After slaying the Horrors, they were suddenly confronted by a new foe, a corrupted Forest Guardian, which they outran through clever use of illusions. Then, you crossed the treacherous swampland between you and your quarry. A fairy of Grief showed you a vision of the deaths that caused these horrors: a horse and her rider were drowned in the barrow atop the hill by masked cultists. Sneaking up the hill, you finally came face to face with the bog horse.

**Session 6 - Confronting the Bog Horse**
The party fought the Horror terrorizing the Witchwood: the Bog Horse. Caset and Bran got up close and personal, Caset catching bog rot from a nasty bite. While Mae decapitated a child ghoul, Lily disguised herself as the horse's owner, Bertrade. There were multiple valiant attempts to extract the arrow jutting out of the horse's side, culminating in Lily pulling it out, and discovering it did nothing. Then they slew the horse, and with it the Witchwood was restored. On the body, they found Billiam's dagger, saddlebags with a sealed letter revealing an attempted coup from over six hundred years ago, and a stylized hand on the arrowhead. They buried the bog horse and--unenthusiastically--the two ghouls.

The new Patron Fairy of the Gleam, Valeorix, thanked the party for their work and broke Bran's loyalty to Wickham. He attempted to barter with Caset for the Maker Blade, but was rejected. Then he extended everyone's boons for the foreseeable future, as well as gifting it to Lily. They became known as Friends of the Gleam.

The party returned to Rosewater without delay and earned their bounty (60 gold pieces). Everyone went to the pub to celebrate. Lily told Mae about how she lost her ear, and discovered that four hundred years had in fact passed since her disappearance. After waking in the pub to very little hangover, the party returned to Marion's Merchant's rest. They found Sigrid relaxing on the porch, her ghoul rot healed, and were invited in for a breakfast of black pudding.

**Session Seven - Dagonix, Fairy of the Dark**
You ate a lovely breakfast of too-raw black pudding, and relaxed a little around town. Fenni traded her bow for a slightly worse version, Mae tried consoling Lily's existential crisis, only for Lily to get kicked out for bringing a sprite indoors. Bran went to pray at the convent, and was asked to bring everyone for a little mission. Upon arrival, Mother Endurance asked you all to help her clear the catacombs of Horrors, so she could reseal the chamber against undead. You ventured into the catacombs, where you met the goblin tweens being mean, found a mausoleum for a Man who broke a fairy curse, and fought the fairy Dagonix. You struck a deal with the Fairy, and the nuns were able to reseal the catacomb's lower levels.
You gathered in Rosewater for your next move. Caset had just finished forging a prosthetic foot for Sigrid, who had already arranged a wagon for your journey to Fenburgh, and was dropping heavy hints about joining. Flandrina the Songstress also asked to join your travels, and shook hands on a safe journey to Fenburgh with her Guild-loan lute. You also agreed to let Sigrid join, only for her to announce she was bringing along a goblin baby.

**Session Eight - Blade Public Relations**
You left Rosewater in high spirits, using the Witchwood as a shortcut to Fenburgh. While Sigrid paid her respects to her dead friends, and Bran considered a reburial with Wickham, Mae fulfilled her patron's wishes by planting a rotten apple at the Witchwood's heart. You reached The Long Night, a fortified inn, before nightfall. There you met refugees who'd fled from a frozen Horror with a penchant for burying people alive, Mae met an old Sentinel friend, and realised gold was a largely useless currency. Before leaving the next day, Sir Lucia asked you to look for the Blade Eduard, who had gone missing while hunting vampires.

You made good progress travelling, helping some refugees by way of gaslighting, and then experiencing unnaturally cold weather right before making camp. Expert collaboration ensured your fire was undetectable, and you had a good rest. Mae's patron expressed approval, Bran reflected on death and desecration, Lily stole Flandrina's notebook to see what she was saying about them, and Fox hunted for field mice.

The next day, you found a horse belonging to a Blade by the roadside, and after following tracks found a delirious Eduard, who was spying on a manor and drenched in blood.

**Session Nine - The Vilani Vampyr**
You took Eduard the Blade back to the wagon, and spent a long rest recovering from the journey and preparing to enter the Vilani Manor. Eduard told you about the vampire, and you immediately got to making stakes. Cleverly, Bran cast protection from good and evil on Eduard, breaking the vampire charm he was secretly under, and which would have turned him against you at the worst possible time. Then you walked down the winding path into the valley and entered the manor. You were received by the servant Gregor and Lady Vilani and her son, Lucien, all of them looking pale and sickly. Jacqueline, the Vilani second child, had apparently fallen into a deep coma. Fenni convinced Lady Vilani to let her examine her, but no magic worked to heal her. At night, you slept in the manor and tried to find out who the vampire was. You found the shredded, rotten corpse of Lord Vilani, and then came upon Lady Vilani and Lucien being fed on by none other than... Henri the stableboy, who had frozen to death in the forest after a botched Vilani hunt. You fought Henri, and very very quickly succeeded in absolutely destroying him. Fenni's arrow dealt the mortal blow, but instead of dying, Henri's body turned to mist.
