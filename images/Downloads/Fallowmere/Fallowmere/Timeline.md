

- **Est. 1000 bR: 	The Exiled Races leave the continent**
Their disappearance is unexplained, and little remains of their settlements and civilisation today. Traces of the [[Exiled Races]] remain in the human gene pool—with the royal family of the Tiger Isles sporting pointy teeth and pointy ears, supposedly—but no living or dead member of any such race has ever been found. 

-	**Est. 500 bR: The [[Trickster]] descends to the world.**
For three days, the sky is an iridescent colourscape. Seawater tastes sweet, milk curdles, and [sprites](Fairies) flood the world, lighting up the night. These sprites will become a fixture of daily life from this date onwards, as greater and lesser fairies alike move into all the wild corners of the world.

-	**Est. 480 bR:	The Justiciar Church condemns witchcraft on the grounds of Unnatural action.**
The spread of budding covens ceases abruptly across the continent, but [witches](Class.md)continue to practice their art in private.

-	**Est. 430 bR:	Emeric of Eastmarch, author of the first magical grimoire, is burned at the stake for crimes against the Reaper.** 
His texts will go on to form the basis for [magician](Class.md) practice. In the following centuries, magicians prove themselves impervious enough to suppression that Justiciar churches cease active persecution.

-	**385 bR:	The province of Karnov, beset by sorcerers after a greater fairy enters [pacts](Class.md) with hundreds of humans at a time, becomes the target of an Imperial purge.** 
Thousands die, and while the fairy in question is eventually captured, news of the possibility of magical pacts spreads to all corners of the Empire.

-	**301 bR:	Following rising tensions across the northern provinces, the Empire commissions a network of sentry towers across key Fallowmere settlements.**
This is one of the final large-scale uses of [[Viridian]] construction material known to current-day historians. While the technology to refine the material remained intact at this point, its decreased use due to expense—as well as the secrecy of the involved architects—would soon reduce viridian to the blademaster’s forge, and a few centuries later, to disuse entirely. 		

-	**280 bR:	After over a century of theological squabbles, the Widow's Order splits from the Justiciar Church.**
Over the next two centuries, [[Lord Reaper]] worship will fragment into Orders, each dedicated to specific aspects of his Image. The Widow's Order—tolerated by its mother order despite its theological difference—remains the most influential. It is dedicated to [The Widow](Reaper%20Aspects.md), an aspect of the Reaper who occupies herself with family, community, and restorative justice.

-	**92 bR:	[[Fallowmere]] revolts against Imperial Rule.** 
Without other provinces to support it, Emperor Carloman II crushes the rebellion within a year's time.

-	**9 bR:  	The First [Reaping](Reaping Season) (3 years)**
The dead rise as [[Horrors]] across the entire Empire for three full years. Hundreds of thousands die, particularly in the densely populated imperial seat, High Valdor. Empress Catarina I, a military genius, mobilises the [[Church|Justiciar Church]] to combat the onslaught, causing great friction within the Church—itself torn on the Lord Reaper's role in the Reaping—but ultimately succeeding in maintaining her hold on the Empire.

-	**2 bR:	The Second Reaping (5 years)**
A third of all humans on the continent are killed by Horrors and the complete collapse that follows their arrival. High Valdor, the Empire's capital, is sealed off, with its inhabitants either killed by undead or starving. Empress Catarina (Now nicknamed ‘the Dawn’) relocates to New Valdor, located to the south-east. Western provinces like Karnov, Hyperia and Oswick are particularly decimated by famine, and largely abandoned.

-	**0	The Fallowmere-Vespertine Rebellion**
Taking advantage of the military collapse of the Empire, rebel governments in the provinces of Fallowmere and Vesper succeed in seceding from their sovereign following a well-timed uprising. Empress Catarina ‘the Dawn’ is slain in the battle of the Three Queens in the spring of Year 1. Her grandson, Emperor Carloman VI, signs a peace treaty in [Redstone Keep](Fallowmere.md), capitulating to the rebellion. The Petty Kingdoms of Fallowmere and Vesper are founded. 

-	**91	The Third Reaping (1 year)**
Less long and bloody than prior seasons, the third [Reaping](Reaping%20Season.md) serves primarily as a warning to the world that Horrors will keep returning. The Empire is, again, decimated. Pirates freely loot the coastline, and form settlements along coves and capes.

-	**92	The Vampires of Dorimare**
Two Horrors rise in Dorimare, a sleepy village on the edge of Fallowmere’s southern woodlands. Unique in their appearance, they quickly gain a mythos of their own. To this day, mothers whisper tales of the Vampires to rebellious children. Dorimare has been abandoned for almost four centuries. [The Vampires](Special%20Horrors.md) have not been slain.

-	**178	The Assassination of a Justiciar Archbishop.**
The murder, potentially by a competing Deacon, spurs the founding of [The Sentinel](Reaper%20Aspects.md) Order, dedicated to the Sentinel, a warrior aspect of the Lord Reaper who wears black plate and an ebony lance, concerned with military power, the rules of war, and righteous fury.

-	**190 	The Fourth Reaping (5 years)**
Catching the Empire amidst a fraught succession war, this [[Reaping Season]] causes levels of death not seen since the second Reaping over 150 years prior. [[Blades]] emerge, founded by a [mysterious Fairy](Special%20Fairies.md), to help isolated regions fight back against Horror attacks.

-	**203	Maker Cults**
In the wake of the fourth Reaping Season, small [[Maker]] cults spring up across the continent, seeing the Reaper as a cruel God who has overstepped and the Trickster as an unreliable demigod, and thus turning to the absent Maker instead. In some regions the Church persecutes them, in others they find a foothold. Fallowmere sees a rise in Maker worship when the Petty Queen Adelie the Strange builds a Maker shrine of her own on a Fallowmere island. Maker clerics exist from this point onwards.

-	**353	The Fifth Reaping (8 years)**
While long and brutal, with The Marches collapsing completely following a Horror epidemic in their city catacombs, humanity is better prepared for Reaping that in years prior. Blades and Sentinels collaborate on occasion to defeat larger threats.

- **358	The Blackhide Aberration is born.**
Along the shore of the Fallowmere, the town of Fenburgh uncovers what it calls Aberrant Lovers in the marsh. A fairy—[Aislinn, Patron fairy of Decay](Special%20Fairies.md)—and a Horror have fallen in love. They inhabit the ruins of a Viridian tower amidst the Mire. Local villagers sometimes seek to banish or kill them, fearing for the coupling to produce offspring, but fail in their efforts. [Fairborn](Class) are particularly common in this area, as are children born with blue-grey hair. 

-	**410	The False Reaping (6 months)**
 Horrors rise in Port Davos and Oswick. Port Davos executes a Blade after he is accused of raising the dead. Oswick's investigation reaches a dead end.
 
-	**420	The Justiciar Church begins its Blade purges**
Public opinion sways against the Blades following the False Reaping, culminating in a purge of their headquarters across most Imperial Provinces. Even Vesper and Fallowmere imprison a few suspected Blades, although—Blades being far more important to rural, lawless states like Fallowmere—none are executed.

-	**497	The Sixth Reaping (6 years)**
Caught in the middle of a naval war with the [[Sea People]]—a pirate fleet whose members cannot be traced to any continent population and are suspected to be from across the Zephyr Sea—the Empire almost collapses under the added strain of a lengthy Reaping. Again, the Empire's seat in New Valdor is sealed off and quarantined, with Emperor Ivan I relocating to Nimore, in Port Davos. A collaboration of Blades and Sentinels succeeds in pushing back the undead.

-	**503	Purgatio Sum**
Churches across the continent declare the Sentinel Order cause of the Reapings. Thousands of Sentinels and Blades are executed by mobs and in local trials.
