- **Mae Tallvigorn** (Sorceress and Swordswoman)

Mae was born in a small village between Lake Glass and Port Solitude. She grew up with stories of the nomads and the barbarians in the White That Wends. She married young, prioritising her husband, their children and her impressive rose garden. However, in the very back her mind she longed for adventure and felt trapped in her role as a caretaker. She became a blade during the last [[Reaping Season]]. During its second year [[Horrors]] decimated her village taking with them her husband, which she was bit sad about, but most importantly, destroying her pride and joy, her precious rose garden which she had been growing for decades. Having everything taken away from her that she thought mattered, she suddenly felt freer then she ever had. She became a Blade soon after, and was the youngest member of her team.

Two or three years ago, a fight with some Horrors goes awry (she is getting older and slower) and she is gravely wounded. The Patron Fairy of Youth and Summer, [[Special Fairies|Idunn]], approaches her in her dying moments and asks to make a deal to save her life. 

Every full moon, she now receives an apple which grants her the strength of a twenty year old. Mae can go two to three full moons without the apple without any serious consequences to her vitality. However, if she does not consume the apple for too long, more serious conditions can occur.



- **Bran Stooke** (Widow Cleric and Monk)

Bran was befallen with a terrible sickness on his seventh year. His mother was visited by the [[Reaper Aspects|Widow]], and somehow convinced her to spare his life in exchange for sending him to one of her many monasteries.

At the monastery he acquired a basic understanding of healing, though to much frustration of the monastery Father August, he never fully mastered the written word. Upon returning home, it became clear the Widow may have done more than simply spare his life, as he had grown from a sickly child to a big and burly man who never so much as caught a cold.

He became an apprentice under the local mortician [[Quentin]], but was appalled at the man's practices. One day, one of the corpses talked back at a particularly nasty joke of Quentin’s. [[Special Horrors|“Old Mabel”]] had always had a witty tongue and quick temper. She sat up and asked Bran if his old master deserved any mercy. A whispered but resolute “no” was Quentin's death sentence.

Bran was haunted by Mabel's resurrection. When an undead horror attacked Root’s Hearth, and all the villagers were fleeing in panic, he felt calm for the first time since that fateful night. He was digging a grave at the time, and simply gave the gooey thing that approached him a good whack at the location he presumed looked most like its head. He was practically treated like a local hero, and people whispered he had secretly received [[Blades]]' training at the [[Reaper Aspects|monastery]]. Soon, he began to believe it himself, while also craving that eerie calmness that had overcome him when facing the monster. One night, he simply stole out and hit the road, looking for more adventure and horrors to dispatch.



- **Caset Bakrouroi** (Maker Cleric and Paladin)

Caset was born to a mining family in one of the villages that dot the [[Map|Green Mountains]], but hated mining and dreamed of being a smith. Eventually, he convinces the town smith, Alison, to let him apprentice with her. His family disapproved, and occasionally, he would get so fed up that he started mining older, deeper shafts. This is where he first encountered [[viridian]]. Alison laughed at his questions and said some metals are beyond the likes of mortals, and that no one has forged a single piece of viridian since the [[Fallowmere Rebellion]]. 
When his father get's Black Lung, pressure on Caset to pick up the family business increased, but instead he was fully immersed into smithing. Alison was gruff and unsociable, but also a patient teacher and, secretly, impressed with his dedication. Local sprites congregate whenever he're at the forge. The earnestness of his drive attracts them. Sometimes, the buckets, picks or ploughs he forges have a spark embedded in them. 

One day, in a nearby village, he met a woman in the local alehouse. She wore the clothes of a travelling nun, but lacked the insignia of the [[Reaper Aspects|Judge or Widow]]. Her eyes have a smoky quality to them, and whenever he looks at her directly he feels giddy.
Eventually, she promised to divulge the location of a Viridian blueprint if he agreed to a bargain. All she wants is his share of the family mining business. Caset agreed.

This was a trick, and he returned home to find his family forced to sell everything they owned to buy back the share of the business. Ashamed yet determined, he fled for the location the nun had shared with him.

- Caset has access to [[Smith mechanics]].


- **Fenni** (Fairy Domain Ranger)

A peasant family's baby, stolen in the night. A couple of days later, the baby is found, but seemingly different. Are their ears pointier? Their eyes changed? Is it the same child or something new entirely? No risks were to be taken, for her village was a religious one. And so the child was left in the woods.

Fenni was born in Fenburgh to Hermanus and Fiona Lindveld, two Justiciar Church members. When she was stolen from her crib by [[Goblin Market|Goblins]] and returned in an altered fashion, they decided to leave the baby, for it might well be a Fairy creature. They were kind enough, however, that the did not choose the Witchwood or the Grim Mire, but rather travelled two weeks to reach the edge of the [[Fairy Domains|Silver Forest]], which they correctly believed to be a safer place for an abandoned child.

First taken in by the woodland creatures, the child was raised by the defenders of the wild places rangers and druids. But as [[Horrors]] began to rise in the land, Fenni felt the calling to defend nature and stand-up against corruption, and naturally took on the mantle of a Blade. Also, she wanted to discover what she in fact was. Was she a changeling? Or simply a changed child?


- **Lily Thorne** (Carnival Rogue)

Lily was born fourteen years before the Fourth Reaping, and by the time the first year of Reaping had passed she had lost both her parents to Horror attacks. Having shown a talent for throwing knives, sleight of hand tricks, music, and general showmanship, she was taken in by a travelling Carnival Troupe called the **Long Jest**. People need entertainment particularly in times of great strife.

Lily spent her teenage years performing in villages and towns with her troupe, donning the overly dramatic stage name “Bladestorm”. Her affinity for whimsy and illusion attracted a particular sprite she calls **Bumblebee**, granting her a little bit of magic. _The deal to keep this magic is to, wherever possible, free captive sprites being used for magician spells._ On the sixth year of Reaping, the troupe was performing near the Grim Mire in Fenburgh when there was a scuffle between the actors and local Church militia. The first body to fall rose instantly as a Horror. The only reason Lily survived was a Blade called **Lodewijk the Fair,** who came to the rescue.

Lily fled [[Fenburgh]], where she was far from welcome. She took the Imperial Road southward, hoping to find shelter and employment in a city like Lange Meer performing in roadside inns. As she was resting in one such inn, however, she overheard a conversation between two travelling Sentinels, who had been hired to kill a Blade causing trouble in Fenburgh. Realising this was likely Lodewijk, the Blade who had saved her life, she decided she needed to travel back and warn him. Believing it the only way to get ahead of the Sentinels, she decided to take a shortcut through the Witchwood. As soon as she entered it, she got terribly lost. She’s been wandering the Wood for a few days now. Since last night, the forest has suddenly grown dark and frightening, and Lily is pretty sure she’s seen a Horror stalking her.

Lily is life optimist, maybe a little kleptomaniac, deadly with the blade and enjoys seeing people smile. She earns a living performing in taverns both as a musician, knife thrower, and pickpocket.

**Sprite - “bumblebee**”- A curious sprite that has been hanging around Lily for quite some time since she freed it from a jar while drunk. It's been named bumblebee because it bumbles around her and Lily has accepted that this Sprite is just a feature of her life now, and trying to talk it into stealth it’s like pulling teeth every God damn time.

**Circus survivor – “Snaggle tooth Jack**”- He always has a scheme and it always goes wrong. After escaping the Horror attack, he and Lily split paths near Fenburgh. Jack thought he could settle there, perhaps. He has always been owing someone money, a clown and bookie, and usually would very much like Lily to help him out with whatever business he had going on.

**Blade – “Lodewijk the Fair”** – Blades are a relatively new profession, having been founded only a few years ago by a mysterious fairy Patron. Lodewijk may well be the first ‘Blade hero’, and there are many songs of his battles against Horrors and saving of many damsels. He’s got a hawk’s nose, an elegant scar on one cheek, and raven black hair. The nickname is apt.

**Lake guardian - “Boudilatis**”- a fey Lily became entangled with during in the Grim Mire, as she was fleeing the Horror attack in [[Fenburgh]]. A night of fun turned quickly into dangerous affair when Lily did not want to spend eternity drowned or whatever he had planned for their further courtship. She escaped, but not without scars. He might still have her left ear somewhere, and is probably still holding either a grudge or a flame, but she has been wary of water ever since.
