#encounter

Medium fey, neutral. A pale, spindling creature with translucent skin showing veins like root systems. Its limbs bend at odd angles, and it moves with an unsettling fluidity. Davon speaks in soft earth-whispers and clicking sounds, tending the deep places where burrowing things belong. It has begun reclaiming the Rosewater catacombs, filling them with loam and living roots.

**Armor Class** 14 (natural armor)  
**Hit Points** 52 (8d8 + 16)  
**Speed** 30 ft., burrow 20 ft.

|**STR**|**DEX**|**CON**|**INT**|**WIS**|**CHA**|
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
|10 (+0)|16 (+3)|14 (+2)|12 (+1)|16 (+3)|14 (+2)|

**Saving Throws** Dex +5, Wis +5  
**Skills** Stealth +7, Perception +5, Nature +3  
**Damage Resistances** poison  
**Senses** darkvision 120 ft., tremorsense 30 ft., passive Perception 15  
**Languages** common 
**Challenge** 5 (1,800 XP)

### Abilities

**Coffin Step.** As a bonus action, Davon can teleport up to 60 feet to any unoccupied space within 5 feet of a coffin, sarcophagus, or tomb it can see.
**Earth Sense.** Davon knows the location of any creature in contact with the ground within 30 feet.

### Actions

**Multiattack.** Davon makes two Earthen Touch attacks.

**Earthen Touch.** Melee Spell Attack: +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 12 (2d8 + 3) bludgeoning damage, and the target's speed is reduced by 10 feet until the end of its next turn as earth clings to them.

**Reclaiming Roots (Recharge 5–6).** Roots burst through the ceiling in a 20-foot radius centred on a point within 60 feet. Each creature in the area must make a DC 13 Strength saving throw. On a failure, they take 14 (4d6) bludgeoning damage and are restrained (escape DC 13). On a success, they take half damage. Restrained creatures take 7 (2d6) bludgeoning damage at the start of each of their turns. The roots last 1 minute or until Davon uses this action again.

### Reactions
**Burrow Away.** When Davon takes damage, it can burrow up to 20 feet without provoking opportunity attacks. It can use this reaction twice per combat.

### Tactics
Davon uses Coffin Step to stay mobile, teleporting between tombs. It opens with Reclaiming Roots to control the battlefield, then makes Earthen Touch attacks. When bloodied, it may try to communicate its purpose or flee deeper into the catacombs where its roots grow thickest.
