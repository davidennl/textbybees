#miniarc
#### The Wounded Blade

**Location:** Imperial Road, midway between Rosewater and Fenburgh

[[Eduard the Blade]] staggers onto the road, clutching a bleeding wound in his side. He's relieved to see fellow Blades (or capable travellers).
There's a vampyr in the manor below—Vilani Manor. He was attacked in the woods while investigating.
**Wound:** Deep puncture marks, recently bandaged.
- **Medicine DC 12:** These are bite marks, not animal claws. Two neat punctures.

**If healed:** Will join the party though he's weakened (fights at disadvantage, half HP).

**The Manor:** Visible from a ridge overlook. Isolated valley location, overgrown gardens, beautiful but decaying.

---
### The Approach
**The Path Down:** Winding trail through overgrown hedges.

**View of the Manor:**
- Two-story stone manor, early renaissance architecture
- East wall completely covered in blooming blue wisteria
- Gardens look wild.
- Stable visible to the north, door hanging open
- Overgrown rose bushes with thorns thick as fingers, stone fountain, picturesque.
- **Nature DC 12:** The wisteria is blooming out of season
- **Perception DC 15:** No bird sounds. The wisteria has no insects on it.

**A Grand Painting** dominates the north wall. **Other Art:**

2. Stag brought down by arrows, still alive and thrashing
3. Winter forest—nobles on horseback, servants beating the bushes
4. A portrait of just the Count, crossbow across his lap, dead rabbits piled at his feet

**Trophy Heads:** Mounted on the walls—three stags (different sizes), two boars, one massive wolf with yellowed teeth and a crushed skull.

**The Staircase:** Grand, sweeping. Dark wood banister.
- **Investigation DC 12:** Dried blood droplets on the third and seventh steps
- **Investigation DC 15:** Scratch marks on the banister post, fresh (within four months)
- **Arcana DC 14:** There's a faint magical aura throughout the house. Reaper magic is more powerful.

## Session 2: The Confrontation
### Midnight Encounter

**Henri:**
- Appears as a pale teenage boy, fourteen or fifteen
- Stable clothes (rough wool shirt, leather breeches, mud-stained)
- Barefoot
- Eyes reflect red in candlelight
- Blood on his mouth

**When he notices the party:**
He looks up slowly. Smiles sadly.
_"More guests. Count Vilani always did love to entertain. But I'm afraid the master of the house is indisposed."

---
### The Confrontation
Henri doesn't immediately attack. He's curious. He's been alone with his rage and grief for four months.

**If asked about Jacqueline:**
His expression softens slightly.
_"She's asleep. I made sure of it. She doesn't deserve to watch what I do to them. She was kind to me. The only one who wept when I died. The only one who tried to stop them."_

**His demand:**
_"If you want to 'save' this house, then finish what I started. Kill the Countess. Kill Lucien. They're guilty. The Count paid his debt. Let them pay theirs."_

---

### Combat: The Thralls

**Henri calls his thralls to defend him:**

From the shadows and upstairs:
- **Countess Margot Vilani** (use **Noble** statblock, AC 15, HP 9, unarmed strikes)
- **Lucien Vilani** (use **Noble** statblock, AC 15, HP 9, carries a letter opener)
- **Eduard** (use **Veteran** statblock, AC 17, HP 45, longsword and shield—fights at disadvantage due to wounds)

**Thrall Behaviour:**
- Defend Henri but don't fight strategically
- Countess and Lucien use improvised weapons (candlesticks, fire pokers)
- All can be knocked unconscious with non-lethal damage
- **If killed, they die permanently**

**Henri's Tactics:**
- Uses Vampire Spawn abilities (bite, claws, charm)
- Focuses on isolating party members
- Tries to grapple and bite
- If reduced to half HP, becomes more desperate
- **If reduced to 0 HP: transforms into mist and flees to basement coffin**

**Environmental elements:**
- Chandelier can be dropped (Athletics DC 14, 3d6 damage in 10-foot radius)

## The Pantry
Spoiled food, broken jars, the reek of rot. The window hatch has been torn wider—bent metal, splintered wood where something was dragged through. Mist pours out through it now, spreading across the grounds.

Stone steps at the back lead down. The mist flows upward from below.

## The Vilani Crypt
A long hall, stone vaulted ceiling. Alcoves line both walls, each holding a stone relief of a dead Vilani, their bones arranged beneath. Paintings above each one.

Everything's been destroyed. Stone faces gouged out, paintings slashed, bones scattered and crushed.

At the far end: Henri's pine coffin. Mist spills from it.

Between the party and the coffin: a section of floor that looks wrong.

---
## The Trap

**Discovery (Investigation DC 12):** The flagstones in a 10-foot section of the hall sit slightly raised. Pressure plates. Step on them and something happens.

**Investigation DC 14:** The mechanism is old but maintained. Someone oiled it recently (Gregor, probably, following old orders).

**What it does:** Step on the wrong stones and iron bars drop from the ceiling, sealing off both ends of the trapped section. You're caged in a 10-foot box. The bars are solid—no squeezing through.

**From above (if they look up):** Small holes in the vaulted ceiling. **Perception DC 13:** Something's up there. Smells like lamp oil.

**What actually happens:** The bars drop, then burning oil pours through the holes. Anyone trapped inside takes 2d6 fire damage immediately, then 2d6 at the start of each of their turns until they escape or the oil runs out (3 rounds).

**The safe path:** Three flagstones in the center don't trigger anything. They're marked, but subtly.

**Investigation DC 15 (or passive Perception 16):** Faint scratches on three stones—worn marks where boots have scuffed the edges repeatedly. Someone who knew the path walked this many times.

**Alternative solutions:**
- Jam the mechanism before triggering it (Thieves' Tools DC 14)
- Trigger it intentionally with weight/objects and wait for the oil to run out
- Wedge something under the bars so they can't drop fully (Athletics DC 15, needs something sturdy like a weapon or shield)
- Jump it (Athletics DC 14, running start gives advantage)
- Climb along the alcoves (Athletics DC 13, but you're disturbing the desecrated remains)

---
## At the Coffin

**Reconciliation:**
To permanently destroy a Horror, the injustice that created it must be acknowledged and addressed. This doesn't always mean fulfilling the Horror's demands.

**The party has options:**

1. **Agree to kill the nobles**
    - Henri dies satisfied
    - The Countess and Lucien remain charmed, helpless
    - Gregor begs them not to
    - If they do this, Jacqueline wakes to find her family dead by the party's hands
2. **Refuse and try to explain justice differently** (Persuasion DC 15)
    - Argue that the Count's death was justice
    - That the Countess and Lucien have suffered
    - That Jacqueline's suffering isn't justice—it's cruelty
    - That Henri deserves to be mourned properly, not to become a monster
    - **If successful:** Henri weeps. _"I just wanted someone to care that I died. I just wanted it to matter."_
3. **Promise to ensure Jacqueline is free** (Persuasion DC 14)
    - Argue that Jacqueline will leave this place
    - That the Vilani legacy of cruelty will end
    - That he can let her go
    - **If successful:** Henri nods. _"She was always kind. Let her wake. Let her leave. Let this place burn."_
4. **Perform proper burial rites** (Religion DC 12)
    - Pray for Henri's soul
    - Acknowledge his death was unjust
    - Promise he will be properly buried
    - **If combined with other options, grants advantage**

**If reconciliation succeeds:**
The enchantment breaks. Upstairs, Jacqueline gasps awake.

---
## Aftermath
**Jacqueline wakes** in her room, confused and weak.

She goes to the dining room and looks at the hunting paintings. At the trophies. She takes down a painting and throws it into the fireplace.

**Her gift to the party:**
She goes to the Count's study (off the dining room) and retrieves a sword from a locked cabinet.
She gives the party **Vilani Fang**: a +1 longsword, silver-chased blade with a wolf's head pommel. The blade is etched with the words _"Swift and Merciful"_.

**Her plan:**
_"I'm going to burn this manor. All of it. Then I'm riding to my aunt's estate in Redstone. She left this family twenty years ago—she knew what they were. Gregor will come with me, if he wants."_

### Henri (Fampyr Horror)
**Medium undead, neutral**

**Armor Class** 15 (natural armor)  
**Hit Points** 82 (11d8 + 33)  
**Speed** 30 ft., climb 30 ft.

**STR** 16 (+3) | **DEX** 16 (+3) | **CON** 16 (+3) | **INT** 11 (+0) | **WIS** 10 (+0) | **CHA** 12 (+1)

**Saving Throws** Dex +6, Wis +3  
**Skills** Perception +3, Stealth +6  
**Damage Resistances** necrotic; bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing from nonmagical attacks  
**Senses** darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 13  
**Languages** Common  
**Challenge** 5 (1,800 XP)

**Regeneration.** Henri regains 10 hit points at the start of his turn if he has at least 1 hit point and isn't in sunlight or running water. If Henri takes radiant damage or damage from holy water, this trait doesn't function at the start of his next turn.

**Spider Climb.** Henri can climb difficult surfaces, including upside down on ceilings, without needing to make an ability check.

**Vampire Weaknesses.** Henri has the following flaws:
- _Forbiddance:_ Cannot enter a residence without invitation
- _Stake to the Heart:_ If a piercing weapon made of wood is driven into his heart while incapacitated, he is paralyzed until the stake is removed
- _Sunlight Hypersensitivity:_ Takes 20 radiant damage when he starts his turn in sunlight. While in sunlight, has disadvantage on attack rolls and ability checks

**Misty Escape.** When Henri drops to 0 hit points outside his resting place, he transforms into a cloud of mist instead of falling unconscious (as per the _gaseous form_ spell). While he has 0 hit points in mist form, he can't revert to his vampire form, and he must reach his coffin within 2 hours or be destroyed. Once in his coffin, he reverts to vampire form and is paralysed until sunset, at which point he regains 1 hit point.

#### Actions
**Multiattack.** Henri makes two attacks, only one of which can be a bite attack.

**Claws.** _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 8 (2d4 + 3) slashing damage. Instead of dealing damage, Henri can grapple the target (escape DC 13).

**Bite.** _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one willing creature, or a creature that is grappled by Henri, incapacitated, or restrained. _Hit:_ 7 (1d6 + 3) piercing damage plus 10 (3d6) necrotic damage. The target's hit point maximum is reduced by an amount equal to the necrotic damage taken, and Henri regains hit points equal to that amount. The reduction lasts until the target finishes a long rest. The target dies if this effect reduces its hit point maximum to 0.

**Charm.** Henri targets one humanoid he can see within 30 feet. If the target can see Henri, the target must succeed on a DC 13 Wisdom saving throw or be charmed by Henri. The charmed target regards Henri as a trusted friend to be heeded and protected. The target obeys Henri's commands and will fight for him. Each time Henri or his companions do anything harmful to the target, it can repeat the saving throw, ending the effect on itself on a success. Otherwise, the effect lasts 24 hours or until Henri is destroyed, is on a different plane of existence than the target, or uses a bonus action to end the effect.
