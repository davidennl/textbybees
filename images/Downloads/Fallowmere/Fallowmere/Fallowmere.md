
The Petty Kingdom of **Fallowmere** sits at the intersection of several distinct cultural groups, as well as containing varied peoples within itself. [[Reaping Season|Reapings]] have caused high rates of migration between provinces and kingdoms over the past few centuries, meaning Fallowmere contains citizens with roots across the entirety of the continent, even if they all consider themselves Fallow. Fallowmere seceded from [[The Empire]] about five hundred years ago, during the [[Fallowmere Rebellion]]. Current primary settlements are: 

-	**Redstone Keep**: the Kingdom capital, the city is built around the Petty Queen's magnificent castle, built entirely of local, red-hued stone. It sits at the point where the Fallowmere lake and Zephyr Sea almost connect, with a port seaside and lakeside. Redstone Keep has almost forty thousand inhabitants.
Redstone is controlled largely by its three largest guilds: The Tailor's Guild, the Fraternity of Landholders, and the Temple District. In truth, the current Petty Queen, Froukje ‘the Strange’, is a wily politician who plays the guilds against one another, forcing a stalemate which allows her to act as mediator.

There is a small goblin market underneath the city's cemetery, although—having been hit hard by the last Reaping—it is miniscule compared to previous centuries.
The College of Songstresses has a large headquarters in a wing of the University, and controls one of the kingdom's largest archives of stories, folklore and music.

-	**Helmstadt**: another large city on the lake's edge. It's the kingdom's primary source of [[Ecology|freshwater pearls]], as well as hosting ferries for travel to the many lake islands. About ten thousand people live here permanently. During yearly [[Carnival]], that number doubles.

-	**The Black Fortress**: a [[Reaper Aspects|Sentinel Order]] keep set in the north-east region of Fallowmere. It hosts a hundred Sentinels and trains new recruits. Surrounding villages have contentious relations with these priests, who offer both risk of and protection from raids.

-	**The Sands**: these are villages along the Fallowmere coast who've profited from ample trade and have formed strong guild connections with Redstone. 
It is said some of the villages are descended from Sea People pirates, who shipwrecked here centuries prior.

-	**The Lumber Realms**: all around the Fallowmere lake are lumber camps. Their inhabitants are foresters, gatherers and hunters, and often clash with local fairies and their human servants. Every now and then, Redstone sends military support to affected towns, pushing back the Fae.

-	**Malachite Mining Villages**: where the [[Map|Summer River]] springs forth from the Green Mountains, rich deposits of Malachite and Serpentine sit close to the surface. They are mined by villages who specialize in ore collection, smelting, and metalwork. Most common are copper, iron and serpentine products. There are also older Imperial mines of what is commonly known as [[Viridian]], a green stone found in many remaining Empire-era buildings across Fallowmere. It is unclear how this stone was processed, as the Empire has lost the technology required for its use.

---

Geographically, Fallowmere inhabits a temperate climate with wet, cold winters.

- To the North: [[Map|The White That Wends]] is a tundra that stretches—potentially—forever. It is inhabited by nomadic tribes, barbarian clans, and small sedentary fishing settlements along the coast. **The White That Wends** borders Fallowmere at the *North River*, a dangerous and fast-flowing body of water that can only be crossed via a bridge or during winter, when some parts of the river freeze over. On the Fallowmere side of the North River bridge stands *The Black Fortress*.


-  the East: **The Green Moutains**, a vast and deadly mountain range rich in malachite and serpentine deposits, separates Fallowmere from its sister *Kingdom of Vesper*. A few passes cross the mountains and are widely used during summer by travellers and merchants. 
  
	*Vesper* is culturally like Fallowmere, and the states are bound together by a military alliance. Where Fallowmere exports are lumber, orchard fruit, copper and freshwater pearls, Vesper's economy relies on wool production, textiles, grain, and chalk.


- To the South: Fallowmere borders [[The Empire]] at the *Summer River*, where the Imperial Provinces or Karnov and Hyperia lie. Karnov, a vast forest, never quite recovered from the Second Reaping, and is currently one of the most dangerous areas in the continent. Hyperia, which is primarily marshland, is experiencing a [[Church|Justiciar religious revival]], causing tensions with the more mildly religious Empire, which has begun suppressing the province with military force.


- To the West: **The Zephyr Sea**. It’s Fallowmere's primary source of trade, connecting it to Imperial cities via maritime routes. 
  
  The sea is home to many dangers. From fairies and ancient monsters, to strange diseases and pirates. Nobody knows what lies across the sea. Perhaps the mysterious [[Sea People]]. Perhaps the hidden home of the [[Trickster]] herself. Every expedition sent out to the sea has been lost.
