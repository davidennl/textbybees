*Gerrit's Farm*: a small cottage on the road to Fenburgh proper. Gerrit is a widower. He lives in the cottage's main room. It has:
- A tiny kitchen, with copper pans that are hung on either side of the window, a bucket of clean water, and an old tin sink.
	- A straw-stuffed bed in a corner, neatly made.
	- A Justiciar effigy over the doorway, depicting the [[Lord Reaper]] in his monk's robe. In his bony hands he holds a blindfold and weighing scales.
	- A shelf with plates and chipped mugs, and iron cutlery.
	- A book of hymns that belonged to Marieke, which Gerrit can't read. If the party opens the book they find a note stuck between the pages (investigation DC 5), an old love letter from Marieke: 
			 *"Dearest Gerrit,* 
			 *By the time you have someone read this I will be gone. Know that meeting you has been the greatest joy of my life. Wherever I go next, I will think of you, my love.* 
			 *Ever yours,* 
			 *Marieke D'Agnet."*
	- An old cupboard, shockingly at odds with the rest of the room's furniture. It was his late wife Marieke's dowry.

- *The Meadow*: a dozen sheep wander in Gerrit's open field. They nibble on valerian plants that have sprung up in clusters at the forest's edge, and have a glossy, smelly wool.
	- There are no footprints other than Gerrit's and the sheep's.
	- One of the sheep has been sheared. The cut is perfectly smooth, and the sheep has a dazed, happy quality. Animal handling/arcana DC12: Either it ate some very funky grass, or it is under the effects of a charm spell.
	- An investigation DC14 roll uncovers a drop spindle dropped in the grass.

 - *A second room* exists in Gerrit's home, but its door is sealed. Gerrit will need to be convinced to let the party inspect it, unless they have proof it's relevant (Persuasion DC12). The room is *Marieke's spinning workshop*, and contains:
		- A spinning wheel, dusty and untouched. The needle, however, is supernaturally sharp. It smells of ozone, as if a bolt of lightning had just struck it, and can be stolen and used as a +1 dagger.
		- A basket full of balls of wool
		- A pile of raw wool, still smelling slightly of sheep.
		- A cracked window, through which a slight draft comes in. It makes the window pane rattle gently.
	
- A perception roll is needed to sense the presence of something supernatural, and then an investigation DC15 reveals a [[Fairies|fairy]] called **Mirabella** hiding in the basket of yarn. 
	- She's a strange, spider-like creature who collects wool and spins it. She's settled in this place because it was a place of making, currently unoccupied. She has a wispy voice and is constantly moving her hands to spin bits of wool.

- Players can attempt to convince the fairy to stop stealing wool if they can get Gerrit to leave a small wool offering for Mirabell every time he shears a sheep. Alternatively, the party can banish the fairy (persuasion/intimidation DC12), although an insight check DC 12 will let them know that unless the room is sealed or otherwise sanctified, other fairies are likely to make their home here too.

	If the players allow Mirabella to stay, she will offer the party a pair of yarn stockings which serve as *Boots of False Tracks*.
