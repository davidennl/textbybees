
**Curses**
1: Any time you lie, a small bell chimes audibly nearby.  
2: You can't step into beams of natural light. It feels like falling into freezing water and forces you to instinctively recoil.  
3: Candles extinguish in your presence. Any larger fire turns green while you are within 30 feet.  
4: Everything you say sounds sarcastic.  
5: Touching anything valuable will make it sprout wings and attempt to escape.  
6: You smell strongly of lavender. Stealth is disadvantaged.  
7: Insects treat you like royalty.  
8: You are filled with static electricity. Your touch is not strong enough to incur damage, but painful enough to jolt someone awake.  
9: You forget everyone’s name the moment they leave your line of sight.  
10: Your nose is fluorescent, as is anything inanimate you touch with your fingers.  
11–20: No curse.

**Boons (D6)**  
1: Amphibian Kiss - Choose one ability score. Once a day, whenever you kiss a furless animal, you gain a +2 bonus to that ability score until the next dawn. This bonus does not stack with similar bonuses.  
2: Inverted Insight - Choose one ability score. If you hang upside down from a tree branch for at least one continuous minute you gain a +2 bonus to that ability score until the next dawn. This bonus does not stack with similar bonuses.  
3: Mirror Touch - Choose one ability score. Once a day, whenever you, using two mirror surfaces, see the back of your own head, you gain a +2 bonus to that ability score until the next dawn. This bonus does not stack with similar bonuses.  
4: Webwalker’s Grace - Choose one ability score. Once a day, whenever you run through a spider web you gain a +2 bonus to that ability score until the next dawn. This bonus does not stack with similar bonuses.  
5: Poet - Choose one ability score. Once a day, whenever you kneel before someone in front of audience, and earnestly recite a poem, you gain a +2 bonus to that ability score until the next dawn. This bonus does not stack with similar bonuses.
6: Shardbinder’s Favour - Choose one ability score. Once a day, whenever you break an object made of glass that belongs to someone else, you gain a +2 bonus to that ability score until the next dawn. This bonus does not stack with similar bonuses.
