
One of [[Fallowmere]]'s more influential Guilds, The *Songstress Guilds* are loosely allied communities of [[Class|Bards]] who collect folklore, music, and other secrets.

- Within noble circles, the *Songstress Guild*s have been known to perform spycraft for pay.
  
- Flandrina, currently residing in [[Rosewater]], is a Songstress Guild member.
