[[Backstories]]

These rules outline your character's ability to forge items, both mundane and inspired. This system is used for creating new items from raw materials.


1. **Basic Crafting**
This is the process for creating any standard (non-magical) weapon or armour found in the Player's Handbook.

> [!Smithing Skill]
>     Requirements
>         A Forge: A suitable location for smithing.
> 
>         Smith's Tools: You must be proficient with these.
> 
>         Materials: Raw metal (ingots, scrap) valued at 50% of the item's market price.
> 
>         Time: One period of downtime (typically 8 hours). Complex items like heavy armor may require 3-5 days of downtime, at the DM's discretion.

To craft the item, you make a Strength (Smith's Tools) check to represent the hard labour of forging.

**Crafting DC Item Type**

| Dice roll | Item                                           |
| --------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| DC 10     | Simple items (daggers, arrowheads, caltrops)   |
| DC 13     | Martial items (longswords, axes, chain shirts) |
| DC 17     | Complex items (greatswords, plate armor)       |


> [!Outcomes]
>     On a Success: The item is successfully created.
> 
>     On a Failure by 4 or less: The work is unfinished. The materials are safe, but the time is lost. You may try again the next day.
> 
>     On a Failure by 5 or more: The materials are ruined. The time and the gold value of the materials are lost.


2. **The Spark of Creation**
A Spark of Creation is a moment of pure, divine inspiration. Briefly, you imagine what the Maker must have felt when they made the world. These sparks are a resource you can find and collect. You might gain a Spark when you:
- Discover a rare, inspiring geological formation (e.g., a perfect geode, a lode of unusual ore).
- Find a unique component on a monster (e.g., the iridescent scale of a basilisk, the heart-iron of a golem).
- Claim a special alchemical ingredient.
- Witness an awe-inspiring act of creation or nature.

Your DM will let you know when you have found and captured a Spark of Creation. You can (typically) only hold one Spark at a time.


3. **Inspired Blacksmithing**
When you are ready to forge an item, you can choose to expend your Spark to attempt to create a unique piece of equipment.

> [!Special Smithcraft]
> 
>         You must meet all requirements for Basic Crafting (Forge, Tools, Materials, Time).
> 
>         You must declare you are expending your Spark of Creation before you make your roll.

This process requires focus and insight, not just labour. You make a Charisma (Smith's Tools) check. If other people are assisting at the forge, you may roll with advantage.

***Inspired Crafting DC: 17***
- On a Success: The item is created. You expend your Spark, and the item is imbued with a special property. Roll 1d6 on the Inspired Creations Table below.
- On a Failure by 4 or less: The item is successfully created, but it is a normal, non-magical version. Your attempt to inspire it fails, but your Spark of Creation is not consumed. You keep your Spark for a future project.
- On a Failure by 5 or more: The divine power is unruly. The materials are destroyed, and the Spark of Creation is lost.

4. **Special Case: The Maker's Blade**
This unique blade hums with a power you don't understand... yet. You can dedicate time to studying its intricate, magical structure.

- ***Unlocking the Blade's Secret***
	During a long rest, you can choose to spend time studying the Maker's Blade. Make a d20 Arcana check, record the result of your roll. You are tracking a cumulative total (adding each new roll to the previous sum).
	*The goal is to reach a cumulative total of 35.*
