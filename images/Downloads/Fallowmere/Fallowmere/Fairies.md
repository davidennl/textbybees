Fairies have existed in the world for over a thousand years, and are the children of the [[Trickster]]. They appeared rarely in the time of the [[Exiled Races]], but following the [[Timeline|Trickster's descent onto the mortal plane]], have prospered. There are four types of fairy, as classified by the [[Magician Emeric of Eastmarch]]:

- *Sprites*. The most common type of fairy, these are ubiquitous in most areas, manifesting as small floating lights which move freely through the world. Sprites tend to be attracted to mischief, promises, or strong displays of emotion. They are repelled by the presence of the [[Lord Reaper]], or the [[Maker]]. 

Mechanically, sprites can fuel most levelled spells, but must be coaxed into assisting. They are also capable of binding promises.

- *Patron Fairies*. These are the fairies who rule [[Fairy Domains]]. They are exceptionally powerful, dangerous, and fickle. While some Patron Fairies are not considered openly evil, none are truly 'friendly'.

- *Lesser Fairies*. These are the manifold fairies who inhabit a Patron's realm, or are otherwise in allegiance to them. They come in all shapes and sizes, powers, and temperaments. Lesser fairies may scheme against their Patron, but cannot openly challenge them.

- *Goblins*. These are a separate class of fairy, untied to Fairy Domains or their hierarchies. Goblins have many of the same abilities as other lesser fairies, but prefer living near mortal settlements, hidden in what are  a [[Goblin Market]]. They value trade, secrets, and hard work.
