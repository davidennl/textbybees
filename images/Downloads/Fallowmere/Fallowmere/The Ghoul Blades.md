### **Dirge-Singer Ghoul (CR 2)**

_Medium undead, chaotic evil_  
**AC** 13 (natural armor) | **HP** 45 (6d8 + 18) | **Speed** 30 ft.  
**STR** 14 (+2) | **DEX** 14 (+2) | **CON** 16 (+3) | **INT** 10 (+0) | **WIS** 10 (+0) | **CHA** 14 (+2)  
**Saves** Wis +2, Cha +4 | **Skills** Performance +4, Intimidation +4  
**Resistances** necrotic | **Condition Immunities** charmed, frightened, paralyzed, poisoned, exhaustion  
**Senses** darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 10 | **Languages** understands Common

**Undead Fortitude.** CON save (DC 5 + damage) to drop to 1 hp instead of 0 unless radiant or crit.
**Claws.** _Melee:_ +4 to hit, 5 ft. reach. Hit: 10 (2d6 + 3) slashing. DC 12 CON save or be paralyzed until end of next turn.
**Dead Man’s Chorus.** (3 per day) _Bonus Action:_ One undead ally within 30 ft. gains advantage on its next attack roll.

Fairy’s end (2 per day) *reaction*. Neutralizes a levelled spell by extinguishing a sprite. The target must roll a DC 12 spellcasting attribute saving throw. On failure, the spell is cancelled. On success, nothing happens.


### **Mist-Touched Ghoul (CR 2)**

_Medium undead, chaotic evil_  
**AC** 14 | **HP** 38 (7d8 + 7) | **Speed** 30 ft.  
**STR** 13 (+1) | **DEX** 16 (+3) | **CON** 13 (+1) | **INT** 9 (–1) | **WIS** 10 (+0) | **CHA** 8 (–1)  
**Skills** Stealth +5 | **Resistances** necrotic, cold  
**Condition Immunities** charmed, frightened, paralyzed, poisoned, exhaustion  
**Senses** darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 10 | **Languages** understands Common

**Phase Step (Recharge 4–6).** Bonus action teleport up to 30 ft. Leaves fog in previous space—creatures starting their turn there take 4 (1d8) cold damage.
**Ambusher.** Advantage on attacks against surprised creatures.
**Claws.** _Melee:_ +5 to hit, 5 ft. reach. Hit: 8 (2d4 + 3) slashing. DC 12 CON save or be paralyzed until end of next turn.
**Shadow Swipe.** _Melee:_ +5 to hit, 10 ft. reach. Hit: 6 (1d8 + 2) slashing. Ignores half cover, doesn't provoke opportunity attacks.


### **Archer Ghoul (CR 2)**

_Medium undead, chaotic evil_  
**AC** 13 | **HP** 40 (6d10 + 9) | **Speed** 30 ft.  
**STR** 12 (+1) | **DEX** 16 (+3) | **CON** 14 (+2) | **INT** 6 (–2) | **WIS** 10 (+0) | **CHA** 5 (–3)  
**Skills** Perception +2 | **Resistances** necrotic, piercing (nonmagical)  
**Condition Immunities** charmed, frightened, paralyzed, poisoned, exhaustion  
**Senses** darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 12 | **Languages** understands Common

**Ghoul Sight.** Sees invisible/hidden creatures within 30 ft. if they are frightened or paralyzed.
**Bane Longbow.** _Ranged:_ +5 to hit, 80/320 ft. Hit: 8 (1d8 + 3) piercing + 4 (1d8) necrotic. *Barbed Shot*. Targets hit by arrow take 1d6 slashing damage if they move >10 ft. before their next turn ends.
**Skewer Volley (Recharge 5–6).** 15-ft. cone of arrows. Each creature in area must make DC 13 DEX save or take 10 (3d6) piercing; half on success.
