# The Penning Duke (CR 5)

## Duke's Carriage
Large object  
AC 15 | HP 60 | Speed 40 ft. (while pulled)  
*Damage Immunities: poison, psychic*  
*Condition Immunities: all*

**Trampling Charge.** When the carriage moves at least 20 feet straight toward a creature and hits with ram on the same turn, target takes extra 9 (2d8) bludgeoning damage. Target must succeed DC 14 Strength save or be knocked prone and run over for another 9 (2d8) bludgeoning damage.
**Ram.** +5 to hit, reach 5 ft. Hit: 13 (2d8 + 4) bludgeoning.

## The Penning Duke
Medium undead, lawful evil  
AC 17 (ancient silver platemail) | HP 58 (9d8 + 18) | Speed 30 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| 14 (+2) | 16 (+3) | 14 (+2) | 12 (+1) | 10 (+0) | 14 (+2) |
*Saves Dex +6, Wis +3*  
*Vulnerabilities: bludgeoning*  
*Immunities: poison*  
*Conditions: exhaustion, poisoned*  
*Senses: darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 10*  
*Languages: Common, Old Imperial*  
*CR 3 (700 XP)*

**Noble's Arrogance.** Advantage on saves against frightened and charmed.
**Carriage Bound.** While inside carriage with more than 30 HP, has three-quarters cover.
**Abandoned Dignity.** When carriage drops to 30 HP or less, Duke and Servant abandon it. Duke draws blade on his next turn.
### Actions
**Multiattack.** Two Longbow or two Decorative Blade attacks.
**Longbow.** +6 to hit, range 150/600 ft. Hit: 7 (1d8 + 3) piercing plus 4 (1d8) necrotic.
**Decorative Blade.** +5 to hit, reach 5 ft. Hit: 6 (1d8 + 2) slashing plus 4 (1d8) necrotic.
### Reactions
**Imperious Command.** When hit by an attack, commands Servant to interpose. Servant uses reaction to move up to speed toward Duke. If it ends within 5 feet, it becomes target instead.

## The Servant
Medium undead, neutral evil  
AC 13 | HP 45 (7d8 + 14) | Speed 40 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| 18 (+4) | 12 (+1) | 14 (+2) | 6 (-2) | 8 (-1) | 5 (-3) |
*Vulnerabilities bludgeoning*  
*Immunities poison*  
*Conditions exhaustion, poisoned*  
*Senses darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 9*  
*Languages understands Common but can't speak*  
*CR 2 (450 XP)*

**Beast of Burden.** Pulls carriage at full speed. Advantage on Strength checks to pull, push, or drag.
**Undead Fortitude.** When reduced to 0 HP, makes Constitution save DC 5 + damage taken (unless radiant or critical). On success, drops to 1 HP.
### Actions
**Multiattack.** Two Slam attacks.
**Slam.** +6 to hit, reach 5 ft. Hit: 8 (1d8 + 4) bludgeoning. Target succeeds DC 14 Strength save or knocked prone.
**Trampling Pull (Recharge 5-6).** While pulling carriage, moves 40 feet straight. Creatures in path make DC 14 Dexterity save: 18 (4d8) bludgeoning and prone on fail, half damage on success.
### Tactics
Duke fires from carriage while Servant charges. When carriage hits 30 HP, both abandon for melee.
### Loot
Silver platemail (decorative, 5 gp), decorative blade (2 gp), fake jewels, ruby ring (ring of the orator (5gp).

# The Barrow Man (CR 4)
Medium undead, neutral evil  
AC 14 (frozen hide) | HP 90 (12d8 + 36) | Speed 20 ft., burrow 15 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| 18 (+4) | 10 (+0) | 16 (+3) | 6 (-2) | 12 (+1) | 8 (-1) |
*Saves Str +7, Con +6*  
*Resistances necrotic; nonmagical bludgeoning, piercing, slashing*  
*Immunities cold, poison*  
*Conditions exhaustion, poisoned, paralyzed*  
*Senses blindsight 60 ft., passive Perception 11*  
*CR 4 (1,100 XP)*

**Ancient Cold.** Creatures starting turn within 5 feet take 5 (1d10) cold. Water within 10 feet freezes.
**Ambush Predator.** Advantage on attacks against creatures that haven't acted in combat. Advantage on first grapple check if surprised target.
**Burial Instinct.** Advantage on Athletics checks to grapple, shove, or restrain. Drags grappled creatures toward soft earth.
**Undead Fortitude.** When reduced to 0 HP, makes Constitution save DC 5 + damage taken (unless radiant or critical). On success, drops to 1 HP.
### Actions
**Multiattack.** Two Frozen Grasp attacks. Both hits on same target grapples (escape DC 15).
**Frozen Grasp.** +7 to hit, reach 5 ft. Hit: 11 (2d6 + 4) bludgeoning plus 7 (2d6) cold.
**Drag Under (Recharge 5-6).** Buries grappled creature. Target makes DC 15 Strength save or pulled prone and buried. Buried creatures take 14 (4d6) bludgeoning at start of each turn and make DC 15 Strength save to escape. Three failures means fully buried and suffocating. Others can use action for DC 15 Strength check to dig them out.
**Salt Preservation (1/Day).** 15-foot radius. DC 14 Constitution save: 21 (6d6) necrotic, half on success. Creatures killed this way cannot be raised as undead.
### Reactions
**Permafrost Grab.** When creature within 5 feet moves away while not grappling, makes Frozen Grasp attack.
### Tactics
Ambush during rest. Target sleepers. Grapple isolated targets, drag away, bury. Requires soft earth or marshland.
### Loot
salt crystal (preservation component), ancient frozen barley grains.

# The Bat Creature (CR 4)
Medium undead, chaotic evil  
AC 15 | HP 75 (10d8 + 30) | Speed 20 ft., climb 40 ft., fly 50 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| 16 (+3) | 18 (+4) | 16 (+3) | 7 (-2) | 14 (+2) | 9 (-1) |
*Saves Dex +7, Wis +5*  
*Skills Perception +5, Stealth +7*  
*Resistances necrotic*  
*Immunities poison*  
*Conditions exhaustion, poisoned*  
*Senses blindsight 60 ft., darkvision 120 ft., passive Perception 15*  
*Languages understands Common but can't speak*  
*CR 4 (1,100 XP)*

**Canopy Stalker.** In forests with overhead cover, Hide as bonus action. Advantage on Stealth in trees.
**Echolocation.** Can't use blindsight while deafened.
**Keen Hearing.** Advantage on Perception checks using hearing.
**Predictable Pattern.** When attacking or using Acid Drool, DM rolls 1d6 to determine retreat position (assign numbers to trees). Players can use action for DC 14 Perception or Investigation check. Success grants advantage on attacks against her next turn and on saves against Acid Drool.
**Fly by.** Doesn't provoke opportunity attacks when flying.

### Actions
**Multiattack.** Two Wing Claw attacks.
**Wing Claw.** +7 to hit, reach 5 ft. Hit: 8 (1d8 + 4) slashing plus 4 (1d8) necrotic.
**Acid Drool (Recharge 4-6).** 10-foot radius below her. DC 14 Dexterity save: 18 (4d8) acid, half on success. Area becomes difficult terrain for 1 minute.
**Swooping Strike (Recharge 5-6).** Flies up to speed in straight line, makes one Wing Claw at any point. Hit requires DC 14 Strength save or knocked prone.
### Bonus Actions
**Retreat to Canopy.** Flies 25 feet up, then Hide.
### Reactions
**Evasive Flight.** When hit by ranged attack, moves 15 feet without provoking. If out of range, attack misses.
### Tactics
Starts hidden in canopy (DC 16 Perception). Hit-and-run: acid from above, swoop, retreat. Never stays in melee. Observant players can predict patterns.
### Loot
Wing-bones (1 gp to alchemist), acid gland (2 gp component).
