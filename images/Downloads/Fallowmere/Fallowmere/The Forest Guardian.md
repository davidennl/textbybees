### _Actions_

**_Greatsword._** _Melee Weapon Attack: +8 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 20 (3d10 + 6) slashing damage plus 9 (2d8) radiant damage._

**_Moonlit Sweep (Recharge 5–6)._** _The Sentinel slashes its blade in a wide arc of shimmering light. Each creature of its choice within 15 feet must make a DC 16 Dexterity saving throw, taking 27 (6d8) radiant damage on a failed save, or half as much on a success._
### _Legendary Actions_

_The Sentinel can take 2 legendary actions, only one at a time at the end of another creature’s turn._

·         **_Detect._** _The Sentinel makes a Perception check._

·         **_Blade’s Reach._** _The Sentinel extends its sword’s glamour; one creature within 10 feet must succeed on a DC 16 Strength save or be shoved 10 feet and knocked prone._

·         **_Cleave (Costs 2 Actions)._** _The Sentinel makes a single Greatsword attack against each creature within 10 feet._
