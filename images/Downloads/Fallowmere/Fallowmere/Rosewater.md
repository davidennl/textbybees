The town has about a thousand people living in and around it. With the stories of Horrors spreading across the countryside, more and more farmer families have moved towards the town. Rosewater’s own have mixed feelings about the influx. [[Reaping Season]] creates a unique precedent for refuge-seeking, and old Justiciar statutes demand care and solidarity with the displaced. At the same time, Rosewater’s future is already uncertain. It’s primary source of income is merchants travelling through the pass. Without the merchants, and with the added influx of starving farmers, it’s uncertain whether the town’s food supplies will last.

-	**Merchant’s stop**
A large building run by a member of the Merchant’s Guild. Marion hosts travelling merchants in her rooms, has fresh horses and oxen for wagons that need them, and mediates villager’s trades with the Guild. The building has an attached stable, and five rooms, two of them common rooms. Marion’s a shrewd yet good-natured woman. She spent her younger years trading between Vesper and Fallowmere, but after an accident on the pass she decided to retire early, acting as a rest point for old colleagues. She doesn’t yet host the incoming farmers, needing an income to survive herself, but she offers cheap hot meals for the destitute.
Current Patrons of the stop are Flandrine, a representative of the [[Songstress Guilds]]. She’s stuck here, caught mid-travel when the Season hit. She plays songs every other evening in the alehouse and is nervous and eager to leave. There’s also the peddler Jakob, and the wounded [[Blades|Blade]] Sigrid.

-	**The Alehouse**
Run by three generations of Barleys, grandmaster and Mistress Barley, and young Berl. The alehouse is the primary social spot for the town, and ambivalent about foreigners.

-	**The Old Orphanage**
Once a Justiciar Orphanage, it’s been repurposed to house displaced farmers and villagers during the Season. Already, tensions and power plays have emerged in the crowded building. Dennis, an old pig’s farmer who has put his hand to playing gang leader, runs the house now. The mayor senses the situation is spinning out of control and can no longer manage the house with the local militiamen.
Dennis wants security. He knows that the major’s priority is the town’s survival and is sceptical that outsiders fit into this plan. His two cronies are Bjorn and Tanner.

-	**The Tower**
An old [[Viridian]] tower. Albert's office is inside, as are his chambers. He lives there with Alfie, his daughter, who runs the magpie messenger post at the top of the tower. Close is the sherrif’s office, where Bartleby lives. In between the two buildings sits the village storehouse, laden with grain. Bartleby runs the small-town militia and is the only one who owns actual armour. He protects the village food supply with his life. Once a city guard in Helmstadt, he’s now an older man who has grown lean with age. Whether or not he and Albert are more than just colleagues is anyone’s guess.

**The Hidden Basement**: An investigation check 15DC finds an old mechanism hidden at the place where floor and wall come together. A tinkerer can repair it, or it can be reactivated with magic. Doing so will open a floor hatch down into the tower's understory. There are old boxes with rusted weapons, the remains of food, barrels of wine, and a scroll repository in the wall. All the scrolls have been eaten away by insects, however, and will turn to dust in one's hand even where they're still intact. The body of a guard is kneeled before a Maker shrine, an arrow jutting from its sternum.
Items that can be recovered here are: 2 potions of health, an old steel helmet, embossed with the seal of Fallowmere, and an ancient shrine to the [[Maker]] (pre-Rebellion).

-	**The Widow Convent**
A small chapel was built by [[Reaper Aspects|Widow clerics]] in the second century after the Rebellion, and over the years has expanded, accommodating a good dozen faithful. The original chapel, a simple hall with pews and an altar, still stands, but it has been enveloped in its expansions. There’s a long rectangular building where the nuns and monks sleep, and a library has filled a low, squat spire that leans against the chapel proper. A few more additions pad the construction: a herbalist’s workshop, a brewery with an extended basement for kegs. On a nearby hill stands a beekeeper’s shed, and straw beehives overlook the valley alongside the chapel themselves.

*Mother Temperance* is the Convent leader. She’s a short, black woman in her late fifties, with a toothy smile and a terrible fondness of brewing (and drinking) mead. She's originally from [[Fallowmere|the Sands]], and has lived in Rosewater for a few decades now. She will be amenable to any plan to get rid of the Horror, and will be willing to put a good word in for the party if they gel with her.

She’s capable of making Sunshine, a heady, tart mead that can lower the threshold of a ghoul-rot save throw by 10 (from 15 to 5). She’s also give any faithful a Widow’s pendant, which can act as a spell focus for a cleric, and can cast turn undead once a day.

The librarian of the Convent is called *Sister Scrupulous*, and she’s a sour woman who is a scholar of some note and an incredibly skilled healer. She’ll ask the party to check up on *Jenny*, who was supposed to bring her fresh chamomile from the woods (all the chamomile plants in the Convent died a few months back).
She will offer books to anyone who does her a favour.

-	**The Cemetery**
The village cemetery is by the road between Rosewater and the Convent, at the foot of the hill. There’s a shrine to the Judge by a couple of elm trees, and all around are old tombstones. 

-	**The Goblin Market**
By a sickly elm sapling and a fresh grave is the entrance to a fledging [[Goblin Market]]. There is a spying hole in the sickly elm, from where tempting Goblin songs are heard. A rusty shovel stands against the tree. Should anyone dig up the grave, they will uncover a earthy passage lit by blue sprite lanterns. Someone has made an effort to cultivate beds of elegant mushrooms, and at the end of the passage is a wooden door of waist height.

Inside lives a *Goblin Warren* made up of three adults: Pit the diggergoblin, Ick the pickergoblin, and *Pebble the Tradesgoblin*. Pit and Ick are only peripherally visible to visitors, busy with their own tasks in the Warren. Pebble mans the Goblin Display, which hosts:

-	A goblin-made lantern. Can light up hidden things, but if it smashes the trapped sprite will curse whoever was holding it last.
-	A stone from the Viridian tower. It’s always a little wet, for some reason.
-	A large spider in a jar, whose venom twists a man's tongue.
-	A tattered cloak, once worn by a Fairborn traveller.
-	A bottle of the Nuns’ mead
-	A magical rope
-	A unicorn’s hair (adds once a day cast of healing word on any string instrument its put on).

Aside from the three adult goblins, the warren has half a dozen youngsters. Most of these are the Goblin equivalent of tweens: Applewort, Beetle, Penny. There is also Wisp, a Goblin toddler who the Warren is seeking to send off, since Pit is tired of carrying him and neither of the other adults can really be bothered.

-	**The Forester’s cottage.**
The old forester, *Jenny*, was turned inside the wood. Entering her hut at the edge of the Witchwood reveals clues of her having been bitten and infected. She’s written a description of events in a journal, using glyphs. She came across a shadowed figure in the forest marshes, which she describes as a woman. At first, she thought it might be a traveller, but now she's not so sure. The woman was travelling towards the [[Map|Fallowmere]]. Jenny spent the next few days scouting. She did not find the woman again, but she did come across an undead beast, which bit her. After this, the writing becomes more chaotic, feverish. The notebook ends with a warning for anyone reading this to leave the shack as soon as possible. Jenny has locked herself in her own basement, but she doesn't know if the door will hold.
A final prayer reads: *“Sweet Trickster, let luck be by my side one last time.”*
