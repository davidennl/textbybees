## The Penning Duke (d4)

**1.** Wheel tracks on the Imperial Road with no hoof prints. Blood spray patterns on both sides of the ruts.

**2.** A carriage passed someone's camp at midnight. No horse sounds. Just footsteps and creaking wheels.

**3.** Arrow stuck in a tree beside the road—good quality, hunting style. Four more bodies found a mile down with similar arrows in them.

**4.** Merchant cart found overturned, driver crushed. His coin purse untouched, still in his belt.

---

## The Barrow Man (d4)

**1.** Frost on the ground near the marsh in late summer. Ten-foot circle of it, with drag marks leading to disturbed earth.

**2.** Someone's brother went to take a piss in the night. Found his boots half-sunk in mud the next morning. No other trace.

**3.** A woman dug herself out of the riverbank, screaming. Clawed hands, dirt under every nail. Died an hour later from cold, though it was warm out.

**4.** Strong smell of sea salt in an area nowhere near the ocean. Ground is soft there.

---

## The Bat Creature (d4)

**1.** High-pitched shrieking from the forest canopy at dusk. Sounds almost human. Lasts about thirty seconds then stops.

**2.** Deep claw gouges in tree trunks, eight to twelve feet up. Bark is shredded downward.

**3.** A logger's body hanging from branches twenty feet up. Throat torn out. No way to climb that high from below.

**4.** Sizzling sound heard from above, then a traveler's cloak started smoking. Burned through to skin—some kind of acid.

---

## The Ancient Skeletons (d4)

**1.** Three sets of tracks, moving in formation. Footprints are too long—whoever made them had feet at least a foot in length.

**2.** Scorch marks on the road in a pattern. No campfire nearby, no lightning strikes. Ground is frozen in spots, burned in others.

**3.** Someone heard words they didn't recognize. Not Imperial, not northern dialect—something older. Three voices speaking in unison.

**4.** Rust-colored stains on the stones near an old Imperial waymarker. Three bodies nearby, drained gray. Bite marks don't match any animal.
