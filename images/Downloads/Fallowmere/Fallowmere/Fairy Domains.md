
**Fairy domains** are areas ruled by a [[Special Fairies|Greater Fairy]], usually a *Patron*. They operate on their own set of rules, particularly when it comes to space and time. Many fairies inhabit a Domain, all of them loyal to its *Patron*, although some are bound to be ambitious, scheming, or mischievous.

Some **Fairy Domains** of note are:

- [[Rosewater|The Witchwood]], held by the Patron Fairy of the Gleam. This is a great wood forever stuck in twilight. Located near the town of Rosewater, to the East of Fallowmere, the Witchwood sits upon the bones of a great battlefield. Currently, its Patron is [[Special Fairies|Camulorix]].

- [[Fenburgh|The Grim Mire]], held by the Patron Fairy of Decay, is a dangerous marsh with many subterranean passages. This is a place of desolation and despair. Wanderers sink easily into quicksand. Sometimes they drown, sometimes they fall into hidden, dying realms hidden far below the earth. The Patron of the Mire is [[Special Fairies|Ainslinn]], who inhabits the ruins of an ancient [[Viridian]] tower. In the tower's basement is a passage into the [[Deep Roads]].

- [[Ecology|The Fallowmere]], held by the Patron Fairy of Fresh Water. Vast and enigmatic, the lake is the largest geological feature of the Fallowmere Kingdom, and produces its most valuable exports. In some places, it runs deeper than the sea. It hides a great many things, and takes great care of its [[Ecology|Fell Seals]], which are considered the Domain's wards. [[Special Fairies|Abenay]] is the Patron Fairy of the Fallowmere.

- [[Map|The Pale Wood]], held by the Patron Fairy of the Battledance. Little is known of this Domain, which sits outside the North border of Fallowmere. It is a desolate, frozen place, where visitors are quickly turned back from. Bears and elks guard its perimeter, and the Patron Fairy is unknown. [[Secrets]].

- [[Map|The Silver Forest]], held by the Patron Fairy of Birth. Relatively speaking, this is a safe Fairy Domain for children and young people to find themselves in, if with jealously guarded borders. It is held by the Patron Fairy *Peccia*.
