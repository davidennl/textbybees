
Horrors, being agents of the [[Lord Reaper]], and thus opposite to the [[Trickster]], present their own challenge to combat with magic.

- Horrors usually have the ability to dampen magic in some way. In the case of [[Special Horrors|powerful Horrors]], this mechanically translates to *lair actions*.

- Horrors can be weakened ahead of combat via [[Reconciliation]]. Usually, this ritual requires a cleric, but [[Blades]] can also perform it under guidance. Reconciliation rituals are shaped by the injustice which brought the creature back, and require either a speech act or an object. Following Reconciliation, a Horror loses some of their power.
